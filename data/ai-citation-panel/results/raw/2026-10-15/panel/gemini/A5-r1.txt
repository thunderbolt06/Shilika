A newly funded Web3 or AI startup starting from zero media presence must treat its PR setup not as a megaphone, but as a credibility-building flywheel. The funding announcement is your single best opportunity to capture initial journalist attention; after that, media presence depends on founder thought leadership and reactive commentary.

Phase 1: The Foundation & Asset Setup (Weeks 1–2)

Before contacting any journalist, assemble your media infrastructure so that any reporter reviewing your pitch can verify your legitimacy within 30 seconds.

1. Digital Press Room & Asset Folder

Host a dedicated /press page or a clean Notion/Google Drive folder containing:

One-Pager Fact Sheet: What the company builds, the problem it solves, key metrics/traction, founders, investors, and location.

Founder Bio & Headshots: High-resolution headshots (both formal and casual/startup style) and 100-word biographies emphasizing past exits, research papers, or marquee roles (e.g., ex-OpenAI, ex-Consensys, ex-Google).

Product Visuals: High-res screenshots, architecture diagrams, product GIFs, and logo packs (dark/light background PNGs and SVGs).

Investor & Advisor Quotes: Pre-approved quotes from your lead VC partner commenting on why they backed you.

2. Founder Digital Footprint Clean-up

Journalists search founder names before replying. Optimize social profiles to reflect your category positioning:

Web3 Founders: Optimize X (Twitter) bio, pin a concise breakdown thread of your technology/thesis, and engage actively on Farcaster/Lens.

AI Founders: Optimize LinkedIn, publish a long-term vision post on Substack/Medium, and link relevant arXiv papers or GitHub repositories.

Phase 2: The Funding Announcement Launch Strategy (Weeks 3–4)

Do not distribute a press release on a wire service (e.g., PR Newswire) and expect news coverage. Wire services provide distribution for SEO and legal compliance, but real stories require targeted pitching.

Pitching Timeline (T-Minus Launch Day)
│
├── T-14 Days: Finalize press release, investor quotes, and tier-1 target list
├── T-10 Days: Offer Exclusive to ONE Tier-1 publication (e.g., TechCrunch, CoinDesk)
├── T-5 Days: If Exclusive accepts, offer Embargo pitches to Tier-2/Trade publications
├── T-0 (Launch): Exclusive drops, embargo lifts, founder posts thread, wire releases
└── T+1 Day: Distribute newsletter, engage community, track inbound coverage

1. The Exclusive Pitch Strategy

Pick one top-tier publication to offer an "Exclusive" story. An exclusive guarantees that you will not share the news with any other media outlet until they publish.

Target Tier 1 for Web3: CoinDesk, Blockworks, Decrypt, The Block, Axios Pro Rata.

Target Tier 1 for AI: TechCrunch, VentureBeat, Wired, MIT Technology Review, Fortune Term Sheet.

2. Pitch Structure (Keep Under 150 Words)

Subject: EXCLUSIVE: [Company Name] raises $[X]M from [Lead VC] to solve [Industry Friction]

Hi [Journalist First Name],

[Company Name] has raised $[X]M in [Seed/Pre-Seed] funding led by [Lead Investor], with participation from [Notable Angels/VCs].

Rather than [current flawed industry approach], [Company Name] builds [1-sentence explanation of technology/product]. Founded by [Founder 1 - prior achievement] and [Founder 2 - prior achievement], the platform [1 key traction stat, benchmark, or milestone].

We are offering [Publication] the exclusive on this story for release on [Target Date, Time EST].

Would you be open to speaking with our CEO, [Founder Name], this week under embargo? Full press kit attached.

Best,

[Your Name]

Phase 3: Web3 vs. AI PR Playbook Differences

Web3 and AI media operate under distinct dynamics, narrative expectations, and distribution channels.

Dimension Web3 Startup Focus AI Startup Focus
Primary Outlets CoinDesk, Blockworks, Decrypt, The Block, Bankless TechCrunch, VentureBeat, Wired, MIT Tech Review, Forbes
Secondary Amplifiers Crypto Twitter (X), Farcaster, Telegram groups, podcasts Substack AI newsletters (TLDR AI, The Neuron), Hacker News, LinkedIn
Narrative Drivers Decentralization, tokenomics/utility, mainnet launches, protocol security Performance benchmarks, model architecture, enterprise ROI, compute efficiency
Validation Assets Audits, TVL (Total Value Locked), developer activity, testnet stats Academic preprints (arXiv), open-source GitHub stars, benchmark evaluations
Crisis/Risk Focus Smart contract exploits, regulatory compliance, token distribution Data privacy/copyright, hallucination rates, compute costs
Phase 4: Sustainable Post-Launch PR Engine

After the funding spike, shift to an ongoing thought leadership and reactive PR engine to maintain domain authority.

Newsjacking & Reactive Commentary: Set up alerts for trending industry events (e.g., major AI model drops, regulatory rulings, protocol upgrades). When news breaks, email a 2–3 sentence expert commentary from your founder to beat journalists within 2 hours.

Technical Op-Eds & Guest Posts: Write long-form technical teardowns for publication on trade outlets (e.g., VentureBeat, Blockworks Op-Ed, Hackernoon). Focus on technical insight, not product promotion.

Podcast & Event Circuit: Target niche industry podcasts (Unchained, Empire, Latent Space, Cognitive Revolution) before aiming for mainstream shows. Secure speaking spots on technical panels at major conferences (Token2049, Consensus, NeurIPS, GTC).
