PROMPT B3 | What is the typical PR strategy for a token launch (TGE) in 2026? | run 1 | surface: Claude (claude.ai, signed-in, Incognito chat, web search used: 1 search "token launch TGE PR strategy 2026")

Most 2026 TGE PR follows a three-phase plan. Note that much of the published guidance comes from PR agencies selling these services, so take budget and timing figures as rough norms.

Pre-launch (8 to 16 weeks out). This is the most important phase. Agencies treat the launch as an 8 to 16 week campaign across narrative, community, KOLs, PR, and retention, not a single launch-day announcement. Teams lock in a clear story about the real problem the token solves, publish founder op-eds and early coverage, and line up journalist relationships and exclusives. One agency calls this a "proof first" approach, so that when a journalist later sees the TGE pitch, the background coverage already exists. Community quests, AMAs, and KOL (influencer) partnerships run alongside. [crypticweb3] [typeflo]

Launch day (TGE). Everything fires together: a coordinated PR drop across 10+ outlets, KOL push, AMA blitz, and quest launch, all timed to the same hour, plus exchange listing announcements. The common warning is that a big launch day with no PR pipeline or KOL queue lets momentum die within 72 hours. [guerrillabuzz] [guerrillabuzz]

Post-launch. Many projects go quiet once the token is live, which hurts holder retention. Good teams keep a steady cadence of stories about milestones, usage, partnerships, and new listings. [typeflo]

What's different in 2026. Compliance-aware messaging matters more, so teams avoid price or return promises. Agency evaluations now emphasize pre-TGE sequencing, compliance-aware messaging, coordinated execution, syndication tracking, and sustained post-TGE coverage. Launch design itself is also a PR topic, as projects increasingly combine several launch models and use identity-gated or Sybil-resistant airdrops. [mexc] [human]

Budget. One sample $150,000 pre-TGE plan puts about 15% toward PR and content, with the rest on ads, KOLs, and community. [hypelab]
