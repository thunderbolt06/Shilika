Short version: at Series A, PR should earn credibility with developers and buyers, not chase big headlines.

What works now

1. Lead with technical proof, not funding news. A funding announcement gets one day of attention. Benchmarks, cost or latency numbers, open-source releases, and real customer results keep working for months.
2. Make your engineers the voice. Founder and engineer blog posts, conference talks, podcasts, and posts on X, LinkedIn, and Hacker News carry more weight with technical buyers than press releases.
3. Target a few right outlets. Pick 5 to 10 reporters and newsletters that actually cover AI infra (trade press, Substacks, technical podcasts). Build relationships with them before you need coverage.
4. Own one clear narrative. The AI infra space is crowded. Pick one sharp point of view (for example, "inference cost is the real bottleneck") and repeat it everywhere.
5. Optimize for AI search. Buyers increasingly ask ChatGPT, Claude, or Perplexity for tool recommendations. Docs, comparison pages, and third-party mentions help you show up in those answers.
6. Use customers as proof. A named customer case study beats any quote from your CEO.

What to skip

Avoid expensive retainer agencies at this stage, vague "revolutionizing AI" messaging, and announcing every small update.

Simple plan

One big moment per quarter (launch, benchmark, or open-source release), steady weekly content in between, and a small set of reporter relationships you keep warm.
