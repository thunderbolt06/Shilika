ANSWER TEXT:
There's no single "best" one, and many "top agency" lists online are sponsored or self-published, so treat rankings with caution. Here's a short list worth contacting:

Korea-focused firms

- Blockchain Marketing Asia (BMA): Uses local expertise in Korean media, community growth, and digital marketing to help international projects establish a presence. It's backed by Creatip, a South Korean marketing agency. [mexc] [seoulz]
- HighCrypto: Covers community building, localization, KOL management, regulatory compliance, PR and media relations, and events for global projects entering Korea. [techbullion]
- FACTBLOCK: A Korean blockchain accelerator and consulting firm that helps global startups enter the market, which is useful if you need strategy plus PR. [seoulz]

Global firms with Korea ties

- MarketAcross: Named official media partner of Korea Blockchain Week 2026, its fourth time in that role. [dailycoin]
- Outset PR: A crypto-native firm that builds its Asia campaigns around media analytics and tracks how stories spread across aggregators, exchange blogs, and KOL channels. [mexc]

How to pick: Ask each for recent Korean case studies and actual coverage links. A good benchmark is placements in outlets like Chosun Biz, Hankyung, The Bell, Maeil Kyungjae, and TechM. Also check they can handle Korean crypto regulations, Naver/Kakao community work, and local KOLs, since paid "sponsored articles" are not the same as earned media. [cryptojobslist]
