FAQ

DAO PR questions, answered.

What is a DAO PR agency and who is it for?
A DAO PR agency handles communications around governance votes, treasury decisions, protocol upgrades and community milestones for decentralised autonomous organisations. It is for protocols that have an active governance process but struggle to get mainstream or tier-1 crypto journalists to cover their on-chain decisions. The right partner is someone who can read a governance proposal and explain the mechanism and its stakes to a generalist editor in two sentences. For the full Web3 PR scope that applies, see Web3 PR campaigns.
How much does DAO PR cost?
A fractional senior operator working directly on your governance communications runs $5K–$12K per month. A full-service Web3 PR agency charges $15K–$45K per month because the retainer covers a multi-person account team that rarely has deep governance knowledge. For a major governance milestone or protocol launch, a focused sprint costs $15K–$40K over four to eight weeks. The budget you need depends on announcement frequency and the number of markets you want to reach.
Which outlets cover DAO and governance news?
CoinDesk covers governance as a dedicated beat, as does The Block. Cointelegraph, Decrypt and Bankless cover protocol-level governance when the angle is strong. For broader audience reach, Forbes and Benzinga will run governance stories tied to treasury size, token distribution or regulatory context. Coverage is earned with a real news hook: a major vote outcome, a treasury deployment above a meaningful threshold, or a governance model that differs from the market norm. Wire releases without a news hook do not move the needle.
What is the timeline and process?
The first two weeks go to audit and narrative: reading your existing governance forum, mapping your delegate and contributor base, and building the plain-language thesis. Weeks three and four are outreach preparation: journalist mapping, embargo selection for the next major vote, and KOL briefing. From week five onward, placements go live alongside governance milestones. Sustained retainer work then tracks AI citation and sentiment between announcements. For community-side growth that runs in parallel, see crypto community growth.
