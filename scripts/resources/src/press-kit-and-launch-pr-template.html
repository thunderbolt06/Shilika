01Press kit checklist

Host it at yourdomain.com/press as a plain HTML page plus a single zip download. Journalists on deadline will not request access to a shared drive.

Words

  • Boilerplate: 50-word and 100-word versions
  • Fact sheet (one page, template below)
  • Founder bios: 50-word and 150-word versions
  • Latest press release, plus an archive of past ones
  • Approved customer or partner quotes, with names and titles
  • Company timeline with dated milestones
  • "How to describe us" note: correct name, capitalisation, category, pronunciation
  • Topics the founder can speak on, with 2 to 3 sample opinions

Visuals

  • Logo pack: SVG and PNG, light and dark, wordmark and icon
  • Founder headshots: high-res, landscape and portrait, plain background
  • Product screenshots at 2x resolution, no browser chrome, real-looking data
  • Short demo video (60 to 90 seconds) or GIFs
  • Architecture or "how it works" diagram for technical reporters
  • Image credits and usage rights stated

Access

  • Named press contact with email and phone, response within 4 business hours
  • Links to verified social profiles, docs and audits (security and Web3)
  • Zip of everything, under 50 MB
  • Page updated within 24 hours of any news

02Boilerplate template

About [Company]

[Company] is a [category in words buyers use] that helps [ICP] [outcome]. [One proof point: customers, usage, audits, notable partners]. Founded in [year] by [founders, one credibility marker each], the company is backed by [lead investors] and based in [city or "remote, with teams in X and Y"]. Learn more at [domain].

Use the same category phrase everywhere (site, LinkedIn, Crunchbase). Journalists and AI assistants copy boilerplates verbatim, so this paragraph becomes how you are described across the web. No superlatives: "leading", "first" and "revolutionary" get cut by editors.

03Fact sheet template

Company name[Legal and brand name, ticker if any]
What it does (one line)[Under 20 words]
Founded / HQ[Year] / [City]
Founders and leadership[Name, title, one credential each]
Team size[Approximate headcount and locations]
Funding[Total raised, latest round, lead investors, date]
Customers / users[Count or named customers you have permission to name]
Key metrics you will share[e.g. TVL, transactions, models served, threats blocked; with date and definition]
Product and pricing[Products, availability, pricing model]
Security and compliance[Audits with links, certifications, bug bounty]
Milestones[3 to 5 dated milestones]
Press contact[Name, email, phone, time zone]

Every number needs a date and a definition ("monthly active wallets, 30-day window, as of [date]"). Undefined numbers get questioned or cut.

04Founder bio template

150-word version

[Full name] is the [title] of [Company], where [he/she/they] [leads what, in one concrete line]. Before founding [Company], [Name] [most relevant prior role and one specific achievement]. [Second credential: research, open-source project, previous exit, notable employer]. [Name] writes and speaks about [2 to 3 topics] and has been featured in [only outlets that actually ran pieces]. [One human detail, optional]. [He/She/They] is based in [city].

50-word version: first two sentences plus the topics line.

Lead with what makes them credible on this story, not chronology. Reporters use bios to decide whether the founder is a quotable expert beyond this announcement.

05Press release, annotated

FOR RELEASE: [Date], [time] [time zone] Or "UNDER EMBARGO UNTIL ...". Pick one and state it in the first line.

[Company] [does specific new thing] to [specific outcome for specific audience]

Headline: subject, verb, news. Under 15 words. If it would make sense on a competitor's release with the name swapped, it is too vague.

Subhead: [The single most important supporting fact: amount, customer, metric, availability]

[CITY], [Date] · [Company], [boilerplate category phrase], today announced [the news in one sentence: who, what, when, where]. Lede: the reporter's whole story should be possible from this paragraph.

[Why it matters: the problem in the market, with a verifiable fact or source you can link]. Context: why now. Cite public sources, not vague claims.

[How it works, in plain language. One concrete example or use case.] Specifics a technical reporter can check.

"[Founder quote with an opinion or insight, not a restatement of the news]," said [Name, title]. Write a quote a journalist would want to use. "We are thrilled" never gets used.

"[Customer, partner or investor quote with a specific result or reason]," said [Name, title, company]. Third-party proof. Approved in writing.

[Availability, pricing, how to access, what comes next].

About [Company]
[Boilerplate]

Media contact
[Name, email, phone] · Press kit: [yourdomain.com/press]

Length: 400 to 600 words. Links: 2 to 3 maximum, to the press kit, product and any audit or data source. Token projects: no price talk, no listing speculation, no return language; legal review before distribution.

06Embargo note template

Subject: Embargoed until [Day, Date, Time TZ]: [Company] [news in 8 words]

Hi [First name],

Following up on your yes to the embargo. Everything below is under embargo until [Day, Date] at [Time] [TZ]. Please do not publish or share before then.

The news: [One sentence]
Why it matters for your readers: [One sentence tied to their beat or a recent piece of theirs]
Attached / linked: press release, fact sheet, images, [data, demo video]
Interview: [Founder name] is available [time windows with TZ]. Reply with a slot and I will send an invite.
Also briefed: [number] other outlets under the same embargo. Be honest about exclusivity.

If anything changes, I will tell you before anyone else.

[Name] · [phone]

Only send embargoed material to reporters who agreed to the embargo in advance. Never assume an embargo is accepted because you wrote it in the subject line. Track who agreed in the Media List Template ("embargo-reliable?" column).

07Launch timeline · T-21 to T+7

T-21Lock the story. Angle memo, announcement date, exclusive vs embargo decision, spokesperson, approvals list.
T-18Assets in draft. Release, fact sheet, bios, visuals, FAQ including hard questions (security, token, competitors).
T-14Media list final. 20 to 40 reporters by beat, each with a recent relevant article and a personal line.
T-12Exclusive offer to your first-choice reporter, with a 48-hour answer window.
T-10Embargo pitches to the wider list (if no exclusive, or once exclusive is placed and the outlet agrees to a wider embargo).
T-7Spokesperson prep. Message house, three proof points, mock interview with tough questions.
T-5Briefings held. Embargo notes and assets sent to everyone who agreed.
T-3Partner and investor kit sent: copy-paste posts, timing, links that go live at T-0.
T-1Final checks. Press page staged, release in the wire tool (if used), social posts scheduled, war-room channel open.
T-0Embargo lifts. Publish release and blog, founder posts, email to list, share coverage as it lands, thank each reporter.
T+1Second wave. Pitch reporters who passed with a new angle: customer story, data, founder op-ed.
T+3Podcasts and newsletters pitched using coverage as proof.
T+7Retro. Coverage log, message pull-through (did they describe you correctly?), referral traffic, AI citation check, lessons.
