/05 UNDER THE HOOD

Built to be read by machines.

Server-side rendered

Most AI crawlers don't run JavaScript. If your content only appears after a React bundle loads, ChatGPT sees an empty page. Ours ship as plain HTML.

Structured data

Schema for Organization, Person, Article, FAQ, HowTo, Service and LocalBusiness, so engines parse who you are, what you sell and where, without guessing.

Answer-first content

Bottom line up front, one idea per paragraph, named entities and real numbers. Written to be quoted, then humanised so it still reads like a person wrote it.

Hallucinated-URL capture

AI assistants invent URLs on your domain that don't exist. We find them in your logs and 301 them to real pages. Free traffic most competitors drop on the floor.

/11 ANSWERS

AI SEO, the questions buyers ask.

What is AI SEO and how is it different from regular SEO?

Regular SEO tries to rank a page in Google's blue links. AI SEO tries to get your brand named and cited inside the answer itself, in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode. The page work overlaps, but AI SEO puts far more weight on branded mentions across the web, answer-first formatting, structured data and pages that render without JavaScript.

Is AI SEO the same thing as GEO and AEO?

Close enough. GEO (generative engine optimisation) and AEO (answer engine optimisation) are two names for the same goal: being the source an AI answer pulls from. We use "AI SEO" because it's what most buyers search for. The GEO glossary entry and the AEO vs SEO playbook go deeper.

How much does AI SEO cost?

Three plans. Strategic Consulting is $1,000 one-time for the audit, 100 ranked queries and a 3-month strategy you run yourself. Strategic Execution is $3,000 per month and includes technical SEO, 100 AI-feed pages and 25 branded placements. Deep Execution is $4,000 per month with 200 pages and 40 placements.

How long until AI SEO shows results?

First content goes live in month one. On shilikajain.com, a brand-new domain ranked #1 for "fractional PR manager crypto" within a month of launch. Bigger curves take longer: Meru Trophy compounded to 27,300 Google clicks over 16 months. AI citations usually trail Google rankings by a few weeks, because the engines need to recrawl and see the same facts repeated elsewhere.

How do you measure AI visibility?

Three lenses. A fixed prompt panel we run across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Google AI Mode, recording whether you're cited, named or missing. Google Search Console for clicks, impressions and position. And AI referral traffic in your analytics, which most dashboards under-count by default. Every result is reported with a number and a link you can check.
