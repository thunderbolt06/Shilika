For a crypto project preparing for a Token Generation Event (TGE), I'd prioritize PR firms that explicitly handle pre-TGE positioning, embargoed media coverage, launch-day announcements, exchange-listing communications, and post-launch reputation management - not just general Web3 marketing.

Based on current web research, these are the strongest candidates to put on your shortlist. One important distinction: a firm specializing in TGE launches is not necessarily a firm specializing in earned media PR; some combine press outreach with paid placements, influencers, and broader marketing.

How I'd choose between them

Earned media and tier-one crypto press -> MarketAcross, GeniusPR

This is a fit-based shortlist, not an independently audited ranking. Much of the available evidence comes from agencies' own service pages and agency-published comparisons, so ask for references and verifiable launch results before committing.

What to ask each firm before hiring
For a pre-TGE engagement, I would request:
Relevant case studies: At least two comparable token launches, with named projects, dates, and links to published coverage.
Named target publications: A proposed list of journalists and outlets, distinguishing genuine editorial outreach from sponsored articles and press-release syndication.
Embargo execution: How they coordinate multiple publications for the same launch window.
Launch timeline: A backward plan covering founder positioning, media briefings, announcement day, and the first 30-90 days after TGE.
Measurement: Earned placements, relevant readership, referral traffic, qualified community growth, and syndication reported separately.
Compliance: A review process for token-related claims, exchange-listing announcements, and jurisdiction-specific communications.
Be cautious of guarantees around token prices, trading volume, exchange listings, or investment returns. A PR agency can coordinate communications; it cannot guarantee market outcomes or editorial decisions.
