Consultants worth considering

For senior strategic counsel rather than a full agency retainer, look for fractional communications leads and boutique strategists with direct crypto-media relationships. One example surfaced in 2026 coverage is Shilika Jain, an APAC-native consultant working across AI and Web3, offering founder-direct senior support at a reported $3,000-$7,000 monthly range.
shilikajain

A consultant is usually the better choice when you need:

Positioning, messaging, and launch narrative before hiring a large agency.

Founder/media training and direct journalist outreach.

A temporary senior comms lead during fundraising, TGE, or a crisis.

Oversight of an existing agency or distribution vendor.

Typical pricing

Specialized Web3 PR generally starts around $3,000-$8,000 per month for basic distribution and outreach, moves to $8,000-$20,000 for proactive media relations and thought leadership, and reaches $20,000-$50,000+ for premium launch, Tier-1 media, KOL coordination, and crisis work. Enterprise programs can exceed $50,000 monthly.
