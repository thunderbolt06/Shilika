The typical 2026 TGE PR strategy is a 6-12 week sequenced campaign, not a launch-day press release: establish a clear utility-led narrative early, warm up media and credible creators, coordinate an embargoed launch-day wave, then sustain coverage for 30-90 days after listing.

+2

Core narrative

Lead with what the token and network actually do, the problem it solves, and why now - not token price or speculative upside. Teams lock a one-sentence thesis, a category positioning, a founder voice, and a compliance-reviewed fact sheet covering tokenomics, vesting, audits, investors, and use of proceeds.
shilikajain
+1

A strong example: "Protocol X lets institutions settle cross-border invoices in minutes using stablecoin rails," rather than "$X token is launching next week."

Pre-TGE: build credibility

Most effective campaigns begin roughly 8-12 weeks before TGE; larger projects with tier-one exchange ambitions may start 90 days or more out.

Launch week: concentrate attention

The launch is treated as a tightly choreographed 24-72 hour window, with a named "war room" responsible for media, exchange liaison, community monitoring, and founder communications.

A typical sequence is:

T-72 hours: educational KOL primers and founder interviews.

T-0: one embargoed tier-one crypto-media anchor, plus coordinated secondary coverage and exchange-listing announcements.

Post-TGE: sustain the story

The most common failure is stopping communications once the token trades. Strong programs continue for at least 14 days of active PR and then shift into a 30-90 day proof cycle built around product milestones, governance proposals, integrations, usage metrics, and transparent progress against the roadmap.
geniuspr
+2

Useful post-TGE milestones include a first governance proposal or utility tutorial around day 30, an on-chain metrics update around day 60, and a public "promised versus delivered" review around day 90.
coinbound

What good PR measures

Impressions alone are a weak KPI. Better measures include named tier-one/tier-two coverage, journalist and analyst sentiment, qualified community or developer inbound, exchange/partnership conversations generated, and whether the project appears in AI-generated answers for priority buyer queries.
shilikajain

Key 2026 differences

Compliance-aware messaging is standard: legal counsel should review claims by market before launch.
shilikajain

Earned media and KOLs are separated: journalists need differentiated stories; creators need education, proof, and timing.

Exchange communications are a PR workstream: listing desks, announcement timing, and content alignment need weeks of lead time.
