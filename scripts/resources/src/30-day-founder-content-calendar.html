30-Day Founder Content Calendar

Thirty prompts that alternate between LinkedIn and X, built around what founders can write better than anyone else: lessons, opinions with evidence, customer language and the work behind the product. Fill in a line under each prompt, batch on Sunday, post on weekdays.

Ground rules

  1. Write as a person, not as the company page. One idea per post.
  2. The first line is the post. If someone reads only that line, did they learn something?
  3. Spend as long replying to others as posting. On both platforms, comments are distribution.
  4. Never post a number, customer name or screenshot without permission and a second pair of eyes.
Best forFounders of Web3, AI and security companies building in public
Time90 minutes a week to batch, 20 minutes a day to reply
CadenceLinkedIn 3 to 4 times a week, X daily (posts or replies)

HOOKS10 hook formulas

A hook is a promise. It earns the click to "see more" only if the post pays it off. Fill the brackets with something specific to you.

#FormulaShape
1The specific number"We talked to [N] [buyers] last month. [N] said the same thing."
2The confession"I was wrong about [belief] for [time]. This changed my mind."
3The contrarian line"[Common advice] is costing [audience] [outcome]."
4The before and after"[Time] ago we [old way]. Now we [new way]. The difference: [one thing]."
5The direct promise"How to [specific outcome] without [common pain], in [N] steps."
6The overheard quote"A [title] told me: '[exact quote]'. They are right, and here is what it means."
7The cost"This mistake cost us [time / money / customer]."
8The list with a twist"[N] things I check before [decision]. Number [N] is the one people skip."
9The question"Why do [audience] still [behaviour] when [better option] exists?"
10The status update"Day [N] of [building / launching X]. What surprised me this week:"

ROUTINEThe weekly batching routine

Sunday or Monday · 90 minutes

  • 15 min: Capture raw material. Scan the week's customer calls, Slack threads, support tickets and notes. List 10 moments worth sharing.
  • 15 min: Match each moment to a calendar prompt and a hook formula.
  • 45 min: Write first drafts for the week. Speak them into a voice memo first if writing is slow.
  • 10 min: Edit: cut the first sentence if it is throat-clearing, cut adjectives, add one specific number or name.
  • 5 min: Schedule. LinkedIn in your buyers' morning, X when your audience is online.

Every weekday · 20 minutes

  • Reply to every comment on your posts in the first hour.
  • Leave 5 substantive comments on posts by buyers, partners or reporters in your space.
  • Save one idea for next week's batch in a single notes file.

Friday · 10 minutes

  • Note which post started the most conversations (not likes). Do more of that format.
  • Log inbound DMs from buyers, investors or press in your CRM.
