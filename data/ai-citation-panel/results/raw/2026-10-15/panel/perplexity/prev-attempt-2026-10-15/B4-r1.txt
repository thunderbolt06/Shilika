Yes - several crypto PR and Web3 marketing firms explicitly build campaigns for the pre-TGE window: narrative seeding, community/whitelist preparation, KOL activation, investor communications, and exchange-listing coordination. The strongest specialist shortlist is Cryptic, Coinband, Lunar Strategy, Omni Agency, ICODA, TokenMinds, Serotonin, and FINPR.

How to choose

For a pure pre-TGE PR mandate, start with Cryptic, Coinband, Lunar Strategy, or Serotonin. If you need an integrated launch operation rather than media relations alone, prioritize Omni, ICODA, TokenMinds, or AP Collective.
