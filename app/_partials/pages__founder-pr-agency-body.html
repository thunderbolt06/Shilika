FAQ

Founder PR questions, answered.

What is founder PR and who is it for?
Founder PR is a deliberate programme of profiles, op-eds, podcast placements and speaking slots that builds the founder's personal reputation as a category authority, separate from but complementary to company PR. It is for founders in Web3, AI and deep tech who want to be cited by name when buyers, investors or conference programme chairs search for the leading voice in their space. The full scope is covered at the founder profiling service page.
How much does founder PR cost?
A fractional senior operator working on founder profiling and op-eds costs $5K–$12K per month. A focused profiling sprint, covering positioning, two to three byline placements and a podcast tier list, runs $15K–$40K over six to eight weeks. A full agency running the same scope typically charges $15K–$45K per month because the retainer funds a multi-person account team rather than the person actually pitching editors.
Which outlets and conferences can you actually get a founder into?
For op-eds and profiles: CoinDesk, Cointelegraph, Decrypt, The Block, Forbes, Blockworks, AI Magazine, Benzinga and Inc42 where the angle warrants it. For speaking: Token2049, EthCC, Consensus, ETHDenver and Davos-adjacent side events. For podcasts: tier-1 crypto and tech podcasts matched to the founder's category, not a mass blast to any show with a feed. Placements depend on a credible angle, not a wire distribution budget.
What does the process look like and how long does it take?
The first two weeks cover a positioning audit and voice framework: what the founder can credibly claim, which outlets and conferences fit, and which op-ed angles have a realistic chance of running. Weeks three to eight cover active pitching, byline drafting and podcast outreach. First placements typically appear in weeks four to six. An ongoing retainer then maintains a quarterly op-ed cadence and a rolling conference pipeline. See the full sprint timeline in the founder profiling sprint playbook.
