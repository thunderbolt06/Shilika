An APAC crypto PR agency runs media placement and narrative strategy specifically for Korea, Japan, Vietnam, China, India and MENA, where the editors, KOLs and community gatekeepers operate in their own languages and news cycles. How much does APAC crypto PR cost?
A fractional senior operator covering APAC runs $5K–$12K/month for sustained retainer work. A defined launch sprint targeting one or two markets costs $15K–$40K over four to eight weeks. A global agency with a localisation add-on typically runs $20K–$50K/month once the regional surcharge is included. KOL amplification in Korea or Japan sits in the nano tier ($200–$1.5K per creator) to mid tier ($10K–$30K) depending on the influencer's reach and the campaign scope.

Korea: BloomingBit, TokenPost, Cobak, Naver Blog crypto communities. Japan: CoinPost, CryptoTimes JP, ZaikoCast podcast circuit. Vietnam: CoinCu, CryptoVN. China: ChainCatcher, Jinse, PANews.
