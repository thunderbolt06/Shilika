/06 ANSWERS

What plant owners ask first.

Do industrial buyers really use ChatGPT to find suppliers?

More every month. AI is still a small share of total traffic (about 0.25% of the average site's traffic in Ahrefs' AI traffic study), but Google's lead over AI assistants fell from roughly 345x to 210x, and the buyers who do use AI arrive with a shortlist already made. Pages that rank in Google are also the pages AI engines read first, so the same build covers both.

We sell through distributors. Does this still make sense?

Yes, if buyers search for your product or application by name. Pages can route RFQs to the right distributor by region, and they make sure your brand, not a reseller's, is the one the AI answer names.

How fast do the first leads come in?

The first 20 pages go live in weeks 3 to 6. Published industrial cases from the wider market show first inbound leads in 12 to 75 days, depending on niche and catalog depth. We report leads by page from the first month they arrive.

Do we need to rebuild our website?

Usually not. We can add the new pages to your current site or a subfolder, as long as they render as plain HTML. If your site can't support RFQ tracking or server-rendered pages, we'll say so in the audit and scope the smallest fix.
