Researched 1s

The typical 2026 TGE PR strategy is a 6-12 week, compliance-aware sequence that builds a credible narrative before launch, concentrates coordinated coverage and creator activity in a narrow launch window, then proves real product traction afterward. It is less about a single press release and more about synchronized earned media, exchange communications, community, and KOL waves.

Core strategy

Lead with utility, not price. Position the token around a clear problem, category, and use case; avoid promises of returns, speculative price talk, or vague "revolutionary" claims.

Launch-day playbook

A disciplined TGE day usually uses a war room with named owners for media, community, exchange liaison, and founder communications. The press release and embargoed coverage land together; official channels publish the confirmed network, contract, claim/distribution details, and listing links; KOL posts are staggered; and the team responds quickly to questions or misinformation.

A practical sequence is:

Embargoed tier-1 feature goes live in the morning.

Official announcement, exchange listing post, and press release publish simultaneously.

Broad KOL and community amplification begins within hours.

Founder hosts a live AMA or X Space to address tokenomics, utility, and launch mechanics.

Educational follow-ups explain staking, governance, bridging, claims, or other actual token functions.

What separates strong launches

Strong 2026 launches start earlier - often 60-90 days out, and six months for projects targeting major exchange listings - because reporters, exchanges, and credible creators need lead time. They also treat PR as proof-building: every claim must be supported by shipped product, auditable tokenomics, real partnerships, and transparent vesting/distribution information.
