There's no independent ranking for this. Most "best of" lists online are written by agencies or marketplaces promoting themselves, so treat them with caution. Here are names that come up often.

Independent fractional consultants
Shilika Jain is a good example of a true solo fractional option. She does fractional PR for Web3 and AI founders, says she has placed 50+ protocols in Forbes, CoinDesk, Cointelegraph, Decrypt and The Block, and previously handled APAC PR at CoinMarketCap. [citation chip: dev]

Marketplaces for vetted fractional talent
MarketerHire claims it can match you with a dedicated crypto specialist in 48 hours for roughly the cost of an agency's mid-tier package. GTM 80/20 matches crypto startups with senior fractional CMOs averaging 13 years of experience, useful if you need broader marketing leadership, not just PR. [citation chips: marketerhire, gtm8020]

Agencies with flexible retainers
Coinbound: founded in 2018, one of the first crypto-only agencies, and it also offers fractional CMO services for Web3 startups.
MarketAcross: a long-running crypto PR firm whose clients include Binance, Polygon, Solana, Aave and Chainlink, at about $10K to $25K a month.
Outset PR: known for a research-driven, structured approach to media strategy.
FINPR: PR-first, with a strong presence in Europe and the Middle East. [citation chip: www.mexc.com +5]

Quick tip: A fractional individual usually makes sense when your bottleneck is strategy or a single channel, not breadth. Before hiring, ask for recent earned (not paid) placements and talk to two past clients. [citation chip: marketerhire]
