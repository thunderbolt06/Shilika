Fractional PR means hiring an experienced PR professional (or a small senior team) part-time, usually on a monthly retainer, to act as your in-house comms lead without the cost of a full-time hire. For Web3 founders, this person typically knows the crypto media landscape, community dynamics, and the regulatory sensitivities around tokens.

What a fractional PR lead usually does for a Web3 startup:

Shapes your narrative and positioning
Pitches crypto and tech media (CoinDesk, The Block, Decrypt, etc.)
Supports launches, funding announcements, and token events
Handles crisis comms (hacks, exploits, depegs, community backlash)
Aligns messaging across X, Discord, Telegram, and founder thought leadership

How it differs from a traditional PR agency:

Fractional PR | Traditional agency
Who does the work | The senior person you hired | Often junior staff after the senior pitch
Cost | Lower, flexible retainer | Higher fixed retainers, often long contracts
Commitment | Easy to scale up or down | Usually 3 to 12 month minimums
Integration | Works like part of your team, in your Slack/Discord | Works as an outside vendor
Web3 depth | Often a crypto-native specialist | Many are generalists learning the space
Capacity | Limited bandwidth | Bigger team for large campaigns or global reach

Bottom line: Fractional PR suits early or mid-stage Web3 teams that need senior strategy, speed, and crypto fluency on a tight budget. A traditional agency makes more sense when you need large-scale, multi-market campaigns and a bigger team behind you.
