For a Web3 startup in 2026, fractional PR typically costs about $3,000-$14,000 per month, while a full-service Web3 PR agency commonly costs $15,000-$45,000 per month - with premium crypto agencies often charging $30,000-$75,000+ and token-launch campaigns $75,000-$250,000+. [chip: shilikajain +2]

What drives the gap
Fractional PR is generally 40-60% cheaper for comparable senior scope because you are paying one senior operator directly rather than funding an agency's account manager, junior staff, operations, and margin. [chip: shilikajain +1]
A fractional engagement usually covers narrative strategy, media pitching, founder preparation, ghostwriting, and launch communications. A full-service agency adds more execution capacity: a dedicated account team, broader content/social/community support, crisis response, multi-market execution, and more simultaneous outreach. [chip: shilikajain +1]

Watch for add-ons
The headline retainer is not always the all-in cost. Common extras include:
- Wire distribution: roughly $400-$900 per release; agencies may add a 20-40% markup. [chip: shilikajain]
- KOL/influencer management: often 15-25% on top of creator fees. [chip: shilikajain]
- Content, op-eds, or ghostwriting: about $1,500-$4,000 per piece when not bundled. [chip: shilikajain]
- Analyst relations: about $2,000-$8,000/month depending on scope. [chip: shilikajain]

Practical choice
Pre-seed to Series A, one main narrative, founder-led PR: fractional PR at $3,000-$7,000/month is usually the better value. [chip: shilikajain +1]
Post-raise, multiple news moments, global media, KOL/community coordination: a full-service agency at $15,000-$45,000/month is more appropriate. [chip: shilikajain +1]
Token generation event, exchange listing, or major protocol launch: budget for a project-based sprint - about $15,000-$40,000 fractional or $75,000-$250,000+ agency - rather than judging either model by monthly retainer alone. [chip: shilikajain +1]

A reasonable budget rule: if your total PR/marketing budget is under about $25,000 per month, fractional or boutique support is generally more cost-effective; above that, an agency's broader team can justify the premium. [chip: marketerhire]
