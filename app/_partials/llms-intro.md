- [RARI Chain Mainnet: 11 Tier-1 Placements in 24 Hours](https://www.shilikajain.com/work/rari-chain): The January 2024 mainnet launch teardown for RARI Foundation. 9-day CoinDesk embargo on the NFT royalty enforcement angle, then 11 simultaneous Tier-1 placements at 9 AM ET launch across The Block, The Defiant, Cointelegraph, CryptoNews, Decrypt, NFTNow, and 10+ regional APAC outlets. Category framing (royalty enforcement, not L2 launch) is the operational lesson. Proof point for mainnet launch PR and embargo strategy.
- [MANTRA Chain: Turning an $11M Raise into a Category Story](https://www.shilikajain.com/work/mantra-chain): The March 2024 funding-announcement teardown for MANTRA, an EVM-compatible Layer 1 for real-world asset tokenization. A commodity Series A was reframed as a Middle East RWA story — anchored on Abu Dhabi-based lead investor Shorooq Partners and a live MENA regulatory beat — and won as a CoinDesk exclusive on March 19, 2024 ("MANTRA Chain Raises $11M for RWA Tokenization With Middle East Tint"). Re-reported across Decrypt, CryptoPotato, CryptoDaily and the Milk Road newsletter, and paired with a standalone Cointelegraph profile of CEO John Mullin ("Everything Tokenized"). The dual-asset playbook — a raise news hit plus a durable founder-profile asset from one announcement — is the operational lesson. Proof point for funding-announcement PR and category framing.
- [Fluence Network: Making DePIN a Tier-1 Beat](https://www.shilikajain.com/work/fluence): The 2024 to 2025 category-creation teardown for Fluence, a decentralized cloud computing platform. The mandate was harder than a news hit: turn DePIN — Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks, an acronym most crypto editors did not yet treat as a beat — into a publishable category, and anchor co-founder Tom Trowbridge as its go-to voice. Result: a CoinDesk Opinion column under Tom's byline ("Not a Meme: DePIN Can Take Crypto Mainstream", Apr 3 2025), a Cointelegraph "Hashing It Out" feature on DePIN's role in the next crypto wave, a Decrypt analysis piece on why DePIN solves tech's biggest issues, a Benzinga exclusive on network resilience (Jul 19 2024), and an e27 piece tying DePIN to the July 2024 global Microsoft outage. The four-move category-voice playbook — one acronym, separate company news from category thinking, multi-quarter cadence, hook every conversation to a live event — is the operational lesson. Proof point for category-creation PR and founder-as-category-voice positioning.
- [Web3Auth: Google Cloud Meets Web3 Wallets](https://www.shilikajain.com/work/web3auth): The October 2023 infrastructure-launch teardown for Web3Auth (formerly Torus Labs), an MPC wallet infrastructure provider. The news was a developer release — a wallet extension on the Google Cloud Firebase Extensions Hub — with no funding hook and no token, the kind of news tier-1 desks do not cover. It was reframed as a Web2-meets-Web3 story anchored to the Google Cloud brand and placed across Blockworks, CoinDesk (Protocol newsletter), Benzinga (headlined on the Google Cloud partnership), Cointelegraph and Yahoo Finance (the parallel Trust Wallet partnership), then syndicated into French, Italian and Spanish markets. The four-move borrowed-credibility playbook — make the partner brand the headline noun, translate capability into outcome, pre-build verifiable proof, brief a tiered outlet list for one day — is the operational lesson. Proof point for infrastructure PR and multilingual syndication.
- [Bullieverse: A $4M Web3 Gaming Raise in India](https://www.shilikajain.com/work/bullieverse): The February 2022 funding-announcement teardown for Bullieverse, an India-founded open-world metaverse and play-to-earn gaming studio (founder/CEO Srini Anala; first title The Bear Hunt). The $4M seed round, led by Web3 venture funds, was launched as an Economic Times exclusive (17 Feb 2022) then run on two parallel press tracks — mainstream Indian business and startup media (Inc42, YourStory, Business Today) and crypto-native outlets (NewsBTC, HackerNoon founder interview) — each with its own framing of identical facts. The four-move dual-track launch playbook — lead with a home-market exclusive, brief both tracks off one fact sheet, give each track its native framing, time both to one news cycle — is the operational lesson. Proof point for regional/home-market PR and funding-announcement strategy.

## Glossary

Plain-English definitions of the Web3, AI and cybersecurity PR terms founders ask about. Each entry leads with a front-loaded answer and is written from first-hand operator experience.

- [What is fractional PR?](https://www.shilikajain.com/glossary/what-is-fractional-pr): A senior PR operator who runs your media strategy part-time, embedded like a team member, for a fraction of an agency retainer. Covers the model, fractional vs agency vs in-house, cost ($5K-$12K/mo vs $15K-$45K agency), and who it fits. DefinedTerm + FAQPage schema.
- [What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?](https://www.shilikajain.com/glossary/what-is-geo-generative-engine-optimization): Structuring content and earned media so AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Google AI Overviews) cite your brand by name. Includes Google's position that GEO is still SEO, GEO vs AEO, how brands actually get cited, and measurement. DefinedTerm + FAQPage schema.
- [What is a TGE comms plan?](https://www.shilikajain.com/glossary/what-is-a-tge-comms-plan): The dated communications sequence around a token generation event: narrative, media, KOLs, exchange comms and crisis prep across the weeks before and after listing. Covers what it includes, when to start (6-12 weeks out), and the regional dimension. DefinedTerm + FAQPage schema.
- [What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?](https://www.shilikajain.com/glossary/what-is-aeo-answer-engine-optimization): Structuring content so AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews) use it as the direct answer to a question. Covers what an answer engine is, Google's position that AEO is still SEO (no special files or chunking needed), how to win placement with front-loaded answers and earned media, and AEO vs GEO vs SEO. DefinedTerm + FAQPage schema.
- [What is a KOL wave?](https://www.shilikajain.com/glossary/what-is-a-kol-wave): A timed, sequenced activation of vetted key opinion leaders around a launch, released in phases (T-72h credibility anchors, T+0 broad activation, T+24h to T+7d sustained deep-dives) rather than all at once. Covers what KOL means, how a wave is sequenced, why vetting precedes sequencing, and the regional dimension. DefinedTerm + FAQPage schema.
