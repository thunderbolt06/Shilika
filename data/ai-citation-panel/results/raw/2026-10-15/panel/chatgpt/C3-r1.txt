Op-ed placement and editorial consultancies: Best when you already know the argument and need help targeting publications and navigating editorial processes.

Which publications should you target?
Choose the outlet based on the argument and the readers you want to influence, rather than prestige alone.
WIRED and MIT Technology Review: For technically grounded arguments about AI safety, capabilities, research, labor and societal impact.
Fortune and Forbes: For AI's economic impact, enterprise adoption, business strategy and the future of work. Verify the specific section's current submission policies.
TechCrunch and VentureBeat: For startup strategy, AI commercialization, industry shifts and technical market perspectives. Confirm whether an outlet accepts unsolicited opinion submissions or prefers commissioned pieces.
For an India-focused founder, consider adding Inc42 and YourStory to the target list.
These are suggested editorial targets, not confirmed placement commitments.
