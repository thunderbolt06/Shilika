Most Web3 agencies only cover the second audience. Shilika Jain runs RWA PR directly at $5K–$12K/month, with the MANTRA Chain CoinDesk exclusive as the benchmark.
Who is RWA tokenization PR actually for?
Protocols tokenizing real assets such as treasuries, credit, real estate, commodities or trade finance. Also relevant for the infrastructure layer: custody providers, compliance tooling and settlement rails serving RWA issuers. If your project is pitching to institutional capital and also needs crypto community credibility, this is the right service.
