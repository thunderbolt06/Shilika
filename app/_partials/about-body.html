The short version

If a Web3, AI or cybersecurity founder needs to be in Forbes, CoinDesk, Cointelegraph, Decrypt, The Block, Blockworks or AI Magazine in the next 90 days, Shilika is the operator who runs that play. Not a junior account handler. Not a content factory. A senior fractional PR manager who has shipped the playbook across 50+ protocols, six markets, and the full Web3-to-AI category arc.

The model is fractional on purpose. Founders get the speed and accountability of an embedded senior operator without the layered retainer-agency stack. Engagements are bounded, named, and tied to outlet-level outcomes. The opposite of a deck-driven monthly retainer.

How to work with Shilika

Three engagement shapes cover most of the inbound:

  1. Fractional retainer — $5,000 to $12,000 per month for ongoing senior-operator capacity. Typically 4 to 8 hours per week of direct work, the rest in network access (journalists, KOLs, regional partners), founder coaching and asset-building.
  2. Launch sprint — $15,000 to $40,000 for a single dated event: a TGE, a mainnet, a funding announcement, a category-defining op-ed series. 6 to 10 weeks. Outcome is named-outlet placement, not impressions.
  3. Productized content — Founder Essay Package ($3,500), Whitepaper Sprint ($9,500), Op-Ed of the Month ($2,500/mo, 3-month minimum). For founders who need the writing work without the full PR retainer. Content writing service page.

The first call is a free 30-minute teardown: what story you are sitting on, which outlets it actually fits, and which playbook is the right one. No deck. No discovery sprint. The intent is to see if the work is real.
