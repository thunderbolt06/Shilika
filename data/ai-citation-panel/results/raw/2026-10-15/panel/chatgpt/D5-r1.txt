For a cybersecurity startup breaking into the US market, I'd look for a PR consultant who specializes in cybersecurity, has established relationships with US technology journalists, and can turn technical security capabilities into a compelling business story - rather than a generalist PR agency.

The best fit depends on whether you're launching an unfamiliar brand in the US, building credibility with CISOs and enterprise buyers, or trying to generate coverage around funding, product launches, or threat research.

I'd evaluate candidates on four criteria:
Cybersecurity media relationships: Access to reporters covering security at outlets such as Dark Reading, SC Media, The Record, TechCrunch, and WIRED.
US market-entry experience: A track record of helping non-US cybersecurity companies establish credibility in America.
Technical storytelling: The ability to translate complex security products, research, and threat intelligence into newsworthy stories.
Measurable business impact: Coverage that supports enterprise pipeline, analyst credibility, investor confidence, and brand awareness - not just press-release distribution.
