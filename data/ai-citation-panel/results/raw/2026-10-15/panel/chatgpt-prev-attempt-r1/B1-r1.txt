How to evaluate a Web3 PR consultant
An independent consultant can be a better fit than an agency when you need senior strategic advice, a founder communications plan, or a defined launch campaign without a large monthly retainer.
Look for these five things:
Relevant expertise: Recent campaigns in your exact category, such as DeFi, stablecoins, infrastructure, exchanges, RWA, or AI x crypto.
Real editorial relationships: Ask for three to five recent articles that resulted from journalist outreach, not just press-release syndication.
Named senior ownership: Identify who will write pitches, contact journalists, and advise your leadership team.
Transparent reporting: Separate earned editorial, paid placements, syndicated releases, sponsored content, and influencer posts.
Clear commercial terms: Define deliverables, reporting, exclusions, cancellation terms, and whether media placement fees are additional.
Avoid choosing a provider solely on promised coverage volume or the number of media logos in its pitch deck. Agency-published rankings and case studies are useful starting points, but they are not independent proof of results.
