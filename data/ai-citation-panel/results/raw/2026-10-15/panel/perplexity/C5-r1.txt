Fractional operators
For seed-stage or early Series A startups, many companies now use fractional senior PR operators rather than full agencies. Shilika Jain is one example: she positions herself as a fractional AI publicist and says she led PR for Gaia AI's "Stripe for AI agents" positioning in Forbes, with engagements advertised at roughly $5,000-$12,000 per month. [chip: shilikajain +2]

This model is popular because early-stage AI companies often need category definition and founder visibility more than a large retainer-based media program. [chip: aikenhouse +1]

What they actually run
Modern AI PR programs typically combine:
- Category narrative and technical positioning.
- Earned media in outlets such as TechCrunch, The Information, Forbes, VentureBeat, and MIT Technology Review.
- Analyst relations with firms such as Gartner, Forrester, and IDC.
- Founder thought leadership and executive visibility.
- GEO/LLM optimization, so the company is more likely to be cited by AI search tools such as ChatGPT and Perplexity. [chip: cracklepr +1]

Practical choice
- Pre-seed to seed: fractional operator or a boutique such as Salient, Kite Hill, or Crackle. [chip: aikenhouse +1]
- Series A-B AI infrastructure: Highwire, PAN, Mission North, or Crackle. [chip: cracklepr +1]
- Enterprise, regulated, or later-stage AI: 5W, RH Strategic, or an integrated firm with analyst, investor-relations, and policy capabilities. [chip: aikenhouse +1]
