What's included in the fractional PR service?
According to her Fractional Head of PR service page, the engagement typically covers:
- PR strategy and positioning: Category positioning, core messaging, journalist mapping and a 90-day coverage calendar.
- Media outreach: Direct pitching to publications such as Forbes, CoinDesk, Cointelegraph, The Block, Decrypt and Blockworks, where the story is a fit.
- Launch and announcement support: Embargo strategy, announcement planning, spokesperson preparation and coordinated media outreach.
- Founder profiling: Op-ed drafting and placement, founder positioning and opportunities to build executive visibility.
- KOL and influencer coordination: Creator briefing and campaign-wave coordination, particularly for Web3 projects.
- APAC market access: Localisation and regional media outreach across markets including India, Japan, Korea, China and Southeast Asia.
- AI search visibility: Tracking whether the company appears in answers from ChatGPT and other AI search tools.

The exact mix depends on the campaign and business goals. She states that coverage depends on credible news hooks; guaranteed media placements are not promised.
