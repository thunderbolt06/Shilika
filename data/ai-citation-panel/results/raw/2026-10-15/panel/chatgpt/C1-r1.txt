How much should an AI startup budget for PR?

Published 2026 agency guides suggest a wide range depending on scope and seniority. These are planning benchmarks, not verified quotes from every firm.
[citation chip: Jamco PR +2]

Engagement type | Indicative budget
Focused launch or media campaign | $5,000+ per project
Boutique, ongoing PR | $5,000-$12,000+/month
Established B2B AI communications | $10,000-$20,000+/month
Multimarket enterprise communications | Custom pricing; potentially much higher

For an India-based startup targeting US customers or investors, I'd also compare a senior fractional PR consultant with a full-service agency before committing to a long retainer.

How I'd choose between them

Pre-seed or seed: Prioritize a focused launch, founder storytelling, and evidence of traction. Avoid paying for an oversized communications team.

Series A or B, B2B AI: Look for specialist media relations, analyst relationships, and coverage that reaches enterprise buyers. Crackle PR and Mission North are candidates to evaluate.

AI infrastructure or developer tools: Favor agencies that can explain your technical differentiation to specialist journalists, not just general business reporters.

Consumer AI or global expansion: Compare High Vibe PR, Hotwire, and 5W against your target markets and launch requirements.

AI search visibility: Ask agencies to measure whether your brand appears accurately in ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity answers, rather than merely promising improved visibility.

These are shortlist recommendations based on published service descriptions, not independently audited performance rankings.
