ICP and Positioning Worksheet

Most messaging problems are positioning problems, and most positioning problems are ICP problems. Work through these pages in order: who exactly you serve, what they would use instead, what only you do, and how to say it so a buyer repeats it.

Before you start

  1. Pull your 10 best customers or design partners: fastest to close, quickest to value, most likely to refer. If you are pre-revenue, use your most engaged pilots.
  2. Have notes from at least 5 buyer conversations open. You are writing down what you heard, not what you hope.
  3. Do the ICP pages alone first, then compare with your co-founder. The disagreements are the work.

PART 2Positioning canvas

Work left to right. Each box depends on the one before it. Positioning is a choice of context that makes your strengths obvious, not a slogan.

1 · Competitive alternatives
What would customers do if you did not exist? Include doing nothing, spreadsheets, in-house builds, generic AI tools, agencies.
2 · Unique capabilities
What you have that the alternatives do not. Features, data, team expertise, integrations, audits, a business model. Be literal.
3 · Value
For each capability: so what? The outcome a customer gets, ideally measurable. "Faster audits" is weak; "ship upgrades without a two-week review queue" is value.
4 · Target customers
Who cares most about that value? This should match or sharpen Part 1.
5 · Market category
The frame that makes your value obvious. Use an existing category plus a qualifier unless you can fund category education.
6 · Relevant trendsOPTIONAL
Trends that make this matter now. Use carefully; trends date quickly and every competitor can claim them.

PART 3Positioning statement

An internal document, not a tagline. If you can fill every blank with something specific and a customer would nod, you are positioned.

Test it. Read it to 5 people in your ICP. Ask them to describe what you do back to you. If their words differ from yours, use theirs. If they compare you to something you did not list as an alternative, add it and redo the canvas.
