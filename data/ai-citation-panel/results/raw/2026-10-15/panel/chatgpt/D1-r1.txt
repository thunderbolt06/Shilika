What should you budget?
Cybersecurity PR retainers vary significantly by scope, seniority, geography, and how much original content or crisis support is included. Use these as initial planning ranges, not verified quotes from the six firms above.
Lean startup program (Targeted media outreach and founder thought leadership): $3k-$7k/mo
Growth-stage PR (Ongoing press, launches, executive visibility): $7k-$15k/mo
Premium or multi-market PR (Senior counsel, broader campaigns, multiple regions): $15k-$30k+/mo
For an early-stage company, I'd start with a three-month pilot, agree on specific deliverables, and avoid paying for a broad PR program before your positioning and news angles are ready.

Five questions to ask before signing
Relevant proof: Which cybersecurity startups have you represented in the last 12-24 months, and what coverage did you earn?
Journalist access: Which reporters cover our specific category - cloud security, identity, application security, threat intelligence, or AI security?
Business impact: How will you measure qualified buyer interest, investor visibility, share of voice, and meaningful coverage?
Technical fluency: Can your team develop stories from original research and technical insights without overstating security claims?
Crisis readiness: Can you support us during a vulnerability disclosure, product incident, or public breach allegation?

One important distinction: if your objective is enterprise sales, prioritize relevant security publications and CISO audiences over raw coverage volume. If your objective is fundraising, make sure the agency can tell a compelling market and founder story in mainstream technology and business media too.
