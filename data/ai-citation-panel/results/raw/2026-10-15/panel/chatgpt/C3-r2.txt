ANSWER TEXT:
For an AI startup founder in 2026, I'd look for a thought-leadership ghostwriting and earned-media agency that can do both parts of the job: develop a distinctive founder POV and secure publication in credible outlets.

The key distinction is that writing an op-ed and getting it placed are different services. A good partner should help with the argument, reporting and drafting, editorial targeting, pitching, revisions, and publication follow-through - not just deliver a polished article.

I'd evaluate three types of partners:
Specialist op-ed and executive communications firms - best for founder-led arguments about AI, business, policy, and the future of work.
Tech PR agencies with contributed-content capabilities - best when the op-ed supports a broader launch, funding announcement, or category-positioning campaign.
Independent ghostwriters with editor relationships - best when you want a founder's voice that feels original rather than corporate.

What I would actually buy
I would start with a six-to-eight-week pilot, rather than a long retainer. Ask for:
One founder interview and a written positioning brief.
Two or three original op-ed concepts tailored to specific editorial desks.
Ghostwriting, fact-checking, revisions and founder approval.
Targeted pitching, follow-ups and editorial coordination.
A report separating submitted, accepted and published pieces.
A placement is never guaranteed by a reputable earned-media provider. Also distinguish genuine editorial publication from sponsored content, paid contributor programs and syndicated press releases.
