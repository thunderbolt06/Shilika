Fractional PR (one senior person, part-time)
Expect roughly $3,000 to $10,000 a month, depending on hours and seniority. That figure is my estimate, based on adjacent data. Extra costs on top of either option

Which fits when
Fractional PR usually makes sense for early-stage teams that need a steady narrative and some founder press. An agency is worth more around a token launch or major raise, when you need wide media relationships and more hands at once.
