Critical Considerations for Launching Web3 in Korea
Factor What to Look For
Naver & Kakao Integration Google and X (Twitter) have lower crypto reach in Korea compared to Naver (for press search indexing) and KakaoTalk / local Telegram channels for community. Your PR partner must index content on Naver.
