Dubai & MENA Web3 PR: costs, firms and what to budget

As of October 2026, the key distinction is between buying media placements, hiring a crypto-specialist PR agency, and running a senior-led regional communications campaign. They can have very different prices, even when all are marketed as "Web3 PR."

For a project entering Dubai, Abu Dhabi or the wider MENA region, I would use the following planning ranges. These are indicative budgets, not verified quotes from every provider.

1. What does Web3 PR cost?
Indicative pricing in USD, based on publicly advertised rates and market guides.
Press-release distribution / placement packages - Useful for announcements and baseline visibility; limited strategic support. - $500-$3K per campaign, indicative
Fractional senior Web3 PR - Founder positioning, targeted pitching and regional media strategy. - $5K-$12K per month
Boutique specialist agency - Ongoing crypto PR, media outreach, messaging and reporting. - $7K-$15K per month
Full-service / larger specialist agency - Broader market coverage, senior strategy, multiple workstreams and sustained campaigns. - $15K-$45K+ per month
Dubai / MENA launch sprint - A focused launch, funding announcement or event-led media campaign. - $12K-$40K per engagement
These ranges are synthesized from published pricing by Shilika Jain and Baden Bower. Jain publishes MENA-specific retainers and launch-sprint rates; Baden Bower advertises a lower-cost, placement-oriented membership model. [chip: Shilika Jain +3]
Important: A guaranteed publication placement is not the same as earned editorial coverage. Ask whether a quote includes agency fees, media fees, influencer/KOL payments, Arabic localization, event support and VAT.

3. What would I budget for a real campaign?
For a Web3 company entering the UAE, I would model the budget around the outcome rather than the number of articles published.
One-off announcement and distribution: $1K-$5K
Founder visibility and targeted media outreach: $5K-$12K/month
UAE market-entry launch: $12K-$35K
Sustained UAE/MENA PR program: $15K-$30K/month
Multi-market, integrated global campaign: $30K-$60K+/month
These are planning estimates synthesized from published provider rates and broader crypto PR pricing guides, not a standardized UAE rate card. [chip: FINPR Agency +2]
For a three-month campaign, that translates into approximately: Lean: $15K-$25K - a focused senior-led program. Mid-market: $30K-$60K - sustained media outreach, founder positioning and launch support. Enterprise: $60K-$135K+ - a larger agency team, broader regional scope and multiple communications workstreams.
Influencer fees, paid sponsorships, major event activations and localization may sit outside these budgets.

4. What should a Dubai/MENA PR brief include?
Before selecting an agency, ask for five concrete deliverables: Named media targets; Regional relevance (UAE investors, business leaders and regulators, including VARA or ADGM where relevant); Earned versus paid coverage; Proof of execution (two or three relevant case studies with live coverage links); Commercial transparency (retainer, minimum term, deliverables, excluded expenses, reporting and cancellation terms).
Do not judge success by a large placement count alone. Prioritize meaningful coverage, journalist relationships, qualified investor or partner attention, and visibility in the markets you actually want to enter.
