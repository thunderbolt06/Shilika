Who Runs Crypto PR in Japan?

The landscape is divided into three primary categories of operators:

1. Native Japanese Web3 & Crypto Agencies

CoinPost / Links: CoinPost is one of Japan's largest Web3 media outlets and runs dedicated agency services, PR distribution, and event-marketing legs.

CryptoTimes / Stake Technologies ecosystem partners: Tech-adjacent marketing and PR teams tied to local ecosystems (like Astar Network's media/community partners) that help foreign protocols localize for Japanese builders and retail investors.

MarketAcross / Baden Bower (Japan Desks): Global PR entities with localized Japanese execution teams to handle press releases, native media placements, and compliance-aware communication.

2. Specialized Regional APAC Operators

DIFY Singapore / MarketAcross / Regional Firms: Regional APAC communications agencies that offer specific "Japan Launch Sprints" by partnering directly with Tokyo-based media, translators, and local Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs).

3. Fractional Senior Operators & Local PR Consultants

Bilingual crypto communications consultants based in Tokyo or Osaka who manage local journalist relationships, ghostwriting, and event activations directly without agency overhead.

Key Additional & Hidden Costs in Japan

Paid Wire & Guaranteed Placements: Sponsored articles or guaranteed releases on outlets like CoinPost or Cointelegraph Japan typically run $2,500 - $5,000 per publication.

Localization & Translation: Translating technical Web3 copy into natural, professional Japanese usually adds $500 - $1,500 per piece if not included in the retainer.

KOL / Influencer Activations: Japanese crypto KOLs on X (Twitter) or YouTube bill separately, ranging from $500 to $5,000+ per dedicated post or video review.
