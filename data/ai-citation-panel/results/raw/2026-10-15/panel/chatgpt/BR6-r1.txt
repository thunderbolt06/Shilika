3. What I would do before signing
Don't choose solely on claimed reach, follower counts, or the number of press placements. Ask each provider for the same information: relevant proof (two recent campaigns at your stage/category); actual coverage links (earned vs sponsored vs syndication); business outcomes (qualified users, developer adoption, partnerships, leads); who does the work; commercial terms (retainer, minimum commitment, creator fees, media-distribution costs, deliverables, cancellation). For token-related campaigns, also check disclosure practices and compliance with advertising and financial-promotion rules. No agency can credibly guarantee token price appreciation or organic virality. [chip: Trusted by Sui, MetaMask & Nexo]
