PR & Marketing Costs in Dubai / MENA
Pricing Model | Cost Range (USD) | What's Included
One-Off News Release / Package | $1,500 - $5,000 | Simple press release drafting, syndication to regional/crypto media (e.g., Cointelegraph MENA, Zawya, Arabian Business), and basic wire distribution.
Monthly Retainer (Standard PR) | $5,000 - $12,000/mo | Dedicated account manager, ongoing media relations, thought-leadership positioning, organic interview pitches, and event/conference press support (e.g., TOKEN2049 Dubai, GITEX, Web3 Summit).
Full-Service Strategy & TGE Launch | $15,000 - $40,000+/mo | Integrated PR, local key opinion leader (KOL) & influencer campaigns (X/Telegram/YouTube), event side-party activations, crisis communications, and localized Arabic translation/PR.

Cost Drivers

Tier-1 Media Guarantee: Securing coverage in mainstream MENA outlets (like Gulf Business, The National, Arab News) or top global crypto outlets incurs higher costs or requires guaranteed placement packages.

Events Premium: Pricing scales during major regional Web3 events (e.g., TOKEN2049 Dubai, Abu Dhabi Finance Week) due to high demand for side-event PR and speaker pitch management.

KOL & Influencer Bundles: MENA campaigns heavily leverage regional crypto influencers across X and Telegram; agency management fees typically add a 15-20% markup on creator budgets.
