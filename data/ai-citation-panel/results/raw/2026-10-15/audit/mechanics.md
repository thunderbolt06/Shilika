# AI answer-engine mechanics: what is empirically known (as of 2026-10-15/16)

Scope: how ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Claude web search, Gemini / Google AI Mode / AI Overviews select and cite sources, with emphasis on B2B-services "who is the best X" and "how much does X cost" queries. This is v2: v1 was written in an earlier run that was cut off by a usage limit (about 34 calls); this continuation run re-opened primary pages that v1 could only see via secondary summaries, corrected two v1 claims, and added four primary sources that v1 missed (Ahrefs best-lists study, Ahrefs 2026-10-12 fan-out study, arXiv 2605.25517 controlled test, CookiesForAI field study).

Evidence ratings used: STRONG = large sample, primary source read, method disclosed, replicated or consistent across independent sources. MODERATE = primary source read but vendor-run observational data, or consistent only across vendor blogs. WEAK = secondary blog, no method, single snapshot, or contradicted elsewhere. UNVERIFIED = I could not open a primary source.

## 0. Method and honesty notes

- Tools: WebSearch (extended mode) and WebFetch only. Ahrefs MCP and Search Console were not used (stated as unavailable). Nothing inside the Shilika project was modified. I did not audit the site itself in this task; statements about the site are inferences from the task description.
- A number is marked READ only when I opened the primary page with WebFetch. Numbers seen only in search summaries are marked SECONDARY. WebFetch answers are produced by a small model summarising the page, so exact figures are as reported by that tool, not copied from the raw HTML.
- Fetch status: in this run READ OK for searchenginejournal.com (llms.txt, SparkToro, schema articles), ahrefs.com (4 pages), developers.google.com, developers.openai.com, arxiv.org abs pages (2 papers), cookiesforai.app, tryprofound.com. Still failed in the earlier run and not retried (so SECONDARY): searchengineland.com (403), sparktoro.com own page (403), seranking.com (tool error).
- Almost every dataset here is produced by a company that sells AI-visibility tooling (Ahrefs Brand Radar, Peec AI, Profound, SE Ranking, Gumshoe, Promptwatch, Evertune, GetCited). Treat all as directional.
- No dataset I found is specific to B2B PR / communications consultancies, or to APAC / Web3 / cybersecurity vendor-selection queries. The closest is Ahrefs' "agencies" slice (finding 14). All inference to that niche is extrapolation.
- Claude was the least studied engine: the only primary-looking evidence is a tiny Profound test (3 queries) plus vendor relays. CookiesForAI did not test Claude.

## 2. Engine by engine

### ChatGPT search
- Retrieval: OpenAI documents OAI-SearchBot as the crawler that surfaces sites in ChatGPT search (finding 12). Several guides say Bing indexing also matters; OpenAI's page does not mention Bing. Treat "must be in Bing" as a practical rule of thumb, not a documented requirement (SECONDARY, WEAK to MODERATE).
- Process (READ, Ahrefs 2026-04-15): the model generates fan-out queries, retrieves a pool, then cites only about half of what it retrieved. Title similarity and descriptive URL slugs correlate with being cited. Reddit URLs make up 67.8% of non-cited URLs, i.e. retrieved but dropped.
- Fan-out in Aug-Sep 2026 (READ, Ahrefs 2026-10-12): only 14.4% of free logged-out responses ran a web search; searching responses averaged about 1.6 fan-outs. "2026" appeared in 17.6% (Aug) to 22.9% (Sep) of English fan-outs; "best" in 5.0% to 5.3%; "official" 10.1% to 13.1%; "price" 6.0% to 8.9% (about +47% relative); the site: operator in 6.8% of fan-outs, 75.9% of those against the roughly 160 most searched sites targeting brand sites. Other studies report far more fan-outs per prompt (Nectiv: 7.61), so counts depend on prompt type.
- Source mix: Wikipedia and Reddit dominate generic citation counts in 2025 vendor data (Profound 680M citations Aug 2024 to Jun 2025: Wikipedia 7.8% of ChatGPT citations; Similarweb Jan-Feb 2026: Wikipedia 13.15%, Reddit 11.97%; SECONDARY). Semrush (SECONDARY via search summary; Semrush page not opened) reported a sharp mid-September 2025 fall in ChatGPT citations of Reddit and Wikipedia over a 13-week, 230,000-prompt tracking window (2025-07-14 to 2025-10-12), and later a Reddit share fall from 3.8% to 0.5%. Ahrefs (READ) puts the March to September 2026 fall at 88%. These aggregate across all topics and say little about B2B-services queries.
- Direction of travel (finding 9): toward official sources and product pages, away from roundups and Reddit. If it holds, it raises the value of the vendor's own substantive pages (pricing, "official", "price" fan-outs) and lowers the value of Reddit and listicle placements on ChatGPT, but it is one snapshot and OpenAI changes retrieval often.
- Variance: highest of any engine I saw measured (SparkToro; CookiesForAI saw 3 different picks in 3 runs). Needs many runs.

### Perplexity
- Own index plus live retrieval. Cites more per answer than ChatGPT (Profound: 21.87 per response, about 4x ChatGPT; another source: 8.2 per response; they disagree, so the count depends on method). SECONDARY.
- Reddit is the top or near-top domain in several vendor datasets (Profound 6.6% of Perplexity citations; Evertune and others 17 to 24% under different denominators); Orbit Media (READ) saw 3% in its last run. No single Reddit share can be trusted.
- Source diversity is high: a vendor analysis says 67% of Perplexity citations fall outside the top 20 domains (SECONDARY, WEAK).
- Freshness (READ, Ahrefs 2025): Perplexity cited URLs are 250 days newer than organic on average. Blog claims such as "3.2x for content under 30 days" have no disclosed method, UNVERIFIED.
- Behaviour (READ, CookiesForAI, WEAK): searched the exact question in 10 of 11 runs and ordered products by its top search results, so it behaves close to a ranked-search reader and a vendor's own "best X" list can place that vendor first.
- Overlap with Google top 10 is higher than other assistants (28.6% vs about 12%, Ahrefs Aug 2025, SECONDARY).
- I found no controlled study isolating Perplexity selection weights. Published "weights" (relevance 30%, authority 15%) are blog estimates.

### Claude web search
- Very likely Brave Search backed (finding 10). Practical consequence: Bing playbooks do not transfer; being in Brave's index and ranking in its top 10 for the query is the best-supported lever, but the backend is not confirmed by Anthropic.
- In one tiny test of five vendor-pair comparisons, Claude cited zero sources in all five (anecdotal, SECONDARY). If Claude often answers "who is best" from model memory rather than retrieval, broad third-party mentions over time matter more than page-level tactics. That is inference, not measured.
- Orbit Media (READ, 3 brands): Claude favoured review sites such as Clutch and kept only 30% of cited URLs over two days.
- Crawlers Claude-SearchBot and Claude-User are named in secondary sources only.

### Gemini, Google AI Mode, AI Overviews
- Google index plus query fan-out (Google doc READ; finding 11). Ahrefs (READ, 2026-03-02): only about 38% of AIO citations come from the organic top 10 for the original query. BrightEdge reports an even lower overlap (about 17%, February 2026; SECONDARY, different method).
- AI Mode leans most on brand-authority signals in the Ahrefs correlation data (branded search volume 0.466 vs 0.352 for ChatGPT).
- Freshness: AIO is the only engine not favouring fresher content (+16 days older than organic).
- CookiesForAI (READ, WEAK): AI Mode and AIO cited Reddit and YouTube in almost every answer plus the same few directory listicles; Gemini (app) was the most run-stable engine (same sources and order in 3 of 3 runs on one question).
- Gemini favours brand-owned sites more than ChatGPT in one vendor analysis (cheers.tech, SECONDARY, WEAK).

## 4. Run-to-run variance and runs needed

- SparkToro/Gumshoe (published 2026-01-30, READ via SEJ): 2,961 runs, 12 brand-recommendation prompts, each run 60-100 times per platform. Identical brand list under 1% of repeat runs on ChatGPT and Google AI (Claude slightly higher); identical ordered list under 0.1%. CORRECTION to v1: 60-100 is how many runs the study performed, NOT a recommendation. SEJ states that how many runs are needed for reliable visibility data is still an open question; the authors' advice is to measure appearance percentage across many queries instead of rank, and to demand that tracking vendors "show their math". Average top-three-brand visibility of about 64% (ChatGPT), 73% (Claude), 68% (Google AI) is SECONDARY.
- Prompt wording varies a lot between people (similarity 0.081 across human-written headphone prompts) but the brand set stays broadly stable (55-77% appearance for the top four headphone brands). The consideration set is measurable; position is not.
- Binomial arithmetic (mine, not from a source): at true appearance rate 0.3, n=60 gives about +/- 12 points of 95% error; n=10 gives about +/- 28. So with 10 runs or fewer a 20% brand and a 60% brand overlap in error; treat such panels as screening only.
- Persistence: Orbit Media (READ) saw 30% (Claude) to 65% (Perplexity) of cited URLs persist over two days. CookiesForAI saw three different ChatGPT picks in three runs while Gemini repeated; engines differ in stability.
- Ahrefs notes AIO fan-out queries are not exposed, and prompt wording changes results (CookiesForAI: adding "open source" replaced the whole AI Mode directory list).

## 5. What is NOT known (explicit gaps)

1. Any study of citation behaviour for B2B services / PR agency / consultant selection and for "how much does X cost" service-pricing queries. Ahrefs' "agencies" slice (finding 14) is the closest and pre-dates the Aug 2026 ChatGPT changes.
2. How often each engine cites a solo consultant's own site for "best X". Own-site share figures range from 4% to 84% depending on definition; none is for services.
3. Whether author / Person entity signals help. No controlled evidence.
4. Whether machine-translated locales help or hurt citations on English queries. No evidence.
5. Anthropic's search provider (no official statement) and OpenAI's use of Bing (no official statement).
6. Whether the 2026 ChatGPT shift away from listicles and Reddit is durable (OpenAI changes retrieval often).
7. How many runs per prompt are enough: unresolved per SparkToro coverage; only the binomial arithmetic above is available.
8. Any replication of the Princeton "up to 40%" on live engines. A July 2026 critical survey (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.14035, SECONDARY, not opened) is reported to find no technique with a stable, longitudinal, cross-platform causal effect.

## 7. Implications for this site (hypotheses to test, not conclusions)

Ordered by how well the evidence supports them. I did not audit the site in this task; the site description comes from the task brief (about 120 playbooks, about 106 landing pages, regional desks, 28 free tools, FAQ schema, llms.txt, /api/markdown routes, 5 machine-translated locales).

1. Retrievability is the only well-documented gate. ChatGPT search needs OAI-SearchBot allowed (OpenAI doc, READ); Google AI features need indexed plus snippet-eligible (Google doc, READ); Claude is probably Brave-indexed (MODERATE to WEAK). Check robots.txt allows OAI-SearchBot, and confirm indexing in Bing, Brave and Google. Cheap and officially documented.
2. Tactics with no supporting evidence or evidence against: llms.txt (300k-domain study: no effect; Google: no AI files needed), FAQ and other JSON-LD (Ahrefs matched test: no lift; Google: no special schema), machine-readable markdown routes (no study found), machine-translated locales (no study found). They are not shown to be harmful but should not be credited for any citation change, and none justifies more effort. Layout-only "answer-first" restructuring also has little effect in the one controlled test (finding 15); specifics (price, statistics, dates) are better supported.
3. Own-site volume is not shown to help. Own-site share figures are all vendor studies not about services. Ahrefs says refreshing or multiplying low-quality pages will not help, and 35% of cited "best" lists sat on low-authority domains, so thin pages are not automatically excluded but are not shown to win either. A large inventory of near-duplicate programmatic and machine-translated pages is a thin-content and spam-classification risk that the research neither proves nor rules out.
4. For "who is the best X" the evidence points off-site: third-party ranked lists (position in the list matters), review sites (Clutch for Claude in Orbit Media's data), LinkedIn, YouTube, and broad brand mentions (Ahrefs correlation about 0.66 for web mentions, about 0.74 for YouTube mentions). A solo consultant's site is unlikely to be the cited source for "best PR consultant" style queries on most engines; the practical lever is being named in pages that are cited. Counter-signal: Ahrefs found self-promotional lists from the recommended publisher present in 17.2% of ChatGPT responses recommending an agency (Dec 2025, finding 14) and Perplexity can place a vendor first from its own "best X" list (CookiesForAI, WEAK), so an honest, dated "best X" page on the site is a testable bet, with the author's own hesitation about it and the August 2026 shift as risks.
5. For "how much does X cost" the best (weak but converging) signals: explicit price information helps in a controlled test; "price" is a rising ChatGPT fan-out term; ChatGPT pulls vendors' own pricing and help pages; official sources are a large share of ChatGPT citations. A specific, honest, quotable pricing page with a visible date is the best-supported own-site lever.
6. Query fan-out means the page that wins is often the one that ranks for a sub-query (for example "pricing", "vs", "official", "for Web3 in Singapore") rather than the head query. Ahrefs READ: only about 38% of AIO citations come from the head-query top 10. Descriptive titles and slugs correlate with ChatGPT citation (READ). Breadth on sub-queries is a rational use of existing page inventory, but only where pages are substantive.
7. Measurement design: with SparkToro's 60-100 runs as the largest disclosed design (not a validated requirement), appearance rate has roughly +/- 12 points of binomial error at p about 0.3 and n=60 (my arithmetic), +/- 28 at n=10. Cited URLs persist only 30% to 65% over two days (Orbit Media). Any before-and-after claim from a few runs per prompt cannot separate effect from noise. Panel run files (data/ai-citation-panel, runs 87 and 88) were not examined in this task. Report per engine, not pooled (only about 11% of domains cited by both ChatGPT and Perplexity per one vendor, SECONDARY, WEAK).
