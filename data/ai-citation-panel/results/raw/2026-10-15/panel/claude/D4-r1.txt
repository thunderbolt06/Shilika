ANSWER TEXT:
For a Series B cybersecurity vendor, plan on roughly $15,000 to $30,000 per month for a specialist PR agency retainer, or about $180K to $360K a year.

How that range is built:

Earlier stage is cheaper. A Series A tech startup should expect to budget between $8,000 and $20,000 per month for a quality PR agency in 2026. [everything-pr]

Series B usually means mid-market scope. Most mid-market companies engaging a national PR agency on a retainer basis spend between $15,000 and $40,000 per month, with project work and crisis engagements priced separately. [everything-pr]

Top-tier firms cost more. $40,000 to $75,000+ per month buys senior partnership from a top-tier national agency. [everything-pr]

Cheaper options exist. General benchmarks put freelance PR at $2,000 to $5,000 and boutique agencies at $5,000 to $15,000 monthly. [swydo]

What pushes cost up:

Cyber specialists (Merritt Group, Highwire, FischTank and similar) charge a premium for CISO-level media and analyst access.
Adding content, analyst relations, awards, or events.
Covering multiple regions.
Big launches like RSAC or Black Hat, often billed as extra projects.

Often billed separately: crisis/breach response, media monitoring tools, wire distribution, and award entry fees.

These are market estimates, so get 2 to 3 agency quotes against a defined scope before deciding.
