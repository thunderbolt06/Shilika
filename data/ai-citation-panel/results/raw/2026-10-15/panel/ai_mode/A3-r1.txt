ANSWER TEXT:
In 2026, fractional public relations (PR) services cost roughly 40% to 60% less than a full-service Web3 PR agency. The core difference lies in paying for the dedicated hours of a single elite operator versus funding the heavy account-team layers and operational overhead of a multi-person firm.
The industry cost benchmarks break down as follows:

What Each Model Delivers
1. Fractional PR ($5K – $12K/month)
With a fractional model, you are hiring a single senior comms strategist who usually takes on no more than 4 to 6 clients at a time.
The Pros: You get 100% senior-level execution. This operator is deeply embedded in your team, understands your complex blockchain infrastructure, and directly pitches tier-1 editors (e.g., CoinDesk, Cointelegraph, Decrypt, Blockworks).
The Cons: Zero backup if they get sick or travel. They lack bandwidth for massive, simultaneous multi-market global launches.
Note on hidden costs: Paid media, sponsored articles, and wire distribution fees (which can run $500 to $3,000+ per release) are always billed separately.

2. Full-Service Agency ($15K – $45K/month)
Agencies operate as a broad, multi-layered machine designed for high execution volume.
[inline source chip: LuvKaizen +1]
The Pros: Tremendous breadth. If your launch requires managing a complex token generation event (TGE), coordinating 30+ crypto Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs), managing localized PR in APAC or LATAM, and running Discord/Telegram moderation simultaneously, an agency provides the necessary army.
The Cons: "Bait-and-switch" staffing. While senior agency executives pitch you during sales meetings, the day-to-day work of drafting and cold pitching journalists is often offloaded to junior account coordinators who may struggle to articulate dense Web3 concepts.
