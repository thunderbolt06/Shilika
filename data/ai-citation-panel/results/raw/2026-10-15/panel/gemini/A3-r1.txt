In 2026, fractional PR services for Web3 startups generally cost $4,000 to $9,000 per month, while full-service Web3 PR agencies range from $12,000 to $25,000+ per month.

Fractional PR provides senior PR strategy and media contacts on a part-time/fractional retainer basis (typically 15–20 hours a month), offering high efficiency without the agency overhead.

Key Differences & Trade-Offs

Fractional PR: Best for pre-launch, Seed, or Series A crypto startups that already have an in-house marketer or content writer to handle drafting and logistics. You pay purely for executive strategy, messaging framing, and direct tier-1 reporter relationships (e.g., CoinDesk, Blockworks, Bloomberg Crypto).

Full-Service Agency: Best for well-funded protocols (Series A/B+) or major token launches requiring heavy event coverage (Token2049, EthCC), continuous press release syndication, influencer management, and large-scale crisis management teams.

Hidden Costs & Add-Ons

Wire Syndication & Distribution: Agencies often bundle standard wire releases (PR Newswire/GlobeNewswire), whereas with fractional PR, you pay the distribution fees directly ($500–$1,500 per release).

KOL / Influencer Marketing: Most PR retainers (both fractional and agency) exclude Web3 influencer budgets. Crypto influencer campaigns typically add $5,000 to $20,000+ per push in performance fees or sponsored content.

Event Activation: On-site PR for major conferences (Token2049, Consensus) often carries a separate project fee ranging from $3,000 to $10,000.
