/01 THE GAP

Great coverage, zero citations.

A pattern we see often in crypto and AI. The team has landed real press, but when you ask ChatGPT about their category, a competitor with worse coverage gets named instead.

Symptom 01

The site is a JavaScript shell

Docs and landing pages built as single-page apps. GPTBot and PerplexityBot fetch the HTML, find almost nothing, and move on.

Symptom 02

The facts disagree

TVL on the site says one thing, the CoinDesk piece another, CoinMarketCap a third. Models skip brands whose numbers don't match across sources.

Symptom 03

No page answers the question

There's a homepage, docs and a blog of announcements. There's no page titled the way buyers actually ask: "X vs Y", "how does Z work", "best tools for W".

/05 ANSWERS

Questions founders ask us.

Can PR alone get a crypto project cited by ChatGPT?

Sometimes, but slowly. Branded mentions are the strongest predictor of showing up in Google's AI Overviews (0.664 correlation in Ahrefs' May 2025 study of about 75,000 brands). Mentions without matching pages on your own site tend to get your competitors cited instead, because the model finds a cleaner answer elsewhere. PR plus answer pages is faster than either alone.

Do AI engines read crypto docs sites?

Only if the HTML has the content in it. Many docs and landing pages render client-side, and most AI crawlers don't execute JavaScript. We check what GPTBot, PerplexityBot and Google-Extended actually receive, then fix the render path or publish server-rendered versions of the pages that matter.

Is AI SEO compliant for token projects?

We write the same way we write press: no price talk, no return projections, no investment framing. Answer pages explain what the protocol does, how it compares and who uses it. That's also what AI engines prefer to quote.
