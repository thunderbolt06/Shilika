PROMPT A4 | Should an AI startup hire a fractional PR consultant or a PR agency? | run 1 | surface: Perplexity (signed-in, Incognito ON, Search mode, default model, free plan)

For most AI startups before Series B, hire a fractional PR consultant first. The usual early-stage problem is defining a credible category narrative and earning a handful of high-relevance placements - not staffing a full press office. A senior fractional operator can provide strategy and direct media outreach at a much lower cost than an agency retainer. [chip: shilikajain +1]

When fractional wins
Choose a fractional consultant if you are seed- to Series A-stage, have a real upcoming milestone - such as a launch, funding announcement, model release, or major customer win - and a founder who can act as spokesperson. Fractional PR commonly costs roughly $3,000-$12,000 per month, versus $15,000-$45,000 per month for a full-service AI/tech agency retainer. [chip: shilikajain +2]
The key advantage is that the strategist who shapes the story is also the person pitching journalists, rather than handing execution to a junior account team. That is especially valuable when your positioning, proof points, and category language are still evolving. [chip: shilikajain +1]

When an agency wins
Hire an agency when you need capacity, breadth, and continuity rather than primarily narrative judgment:
- You are Series B or later, with an in-house communications lead who can direct the agency.
- You are running simultaneous launches across markets, verticals, or regions.
- You need analyst relations, executive visibility, event support, media training, and a sustained newsroom cadence.
- You need crisis-communications bench strength or coverage across many reporter beats. [chip: shilikajain +2]
A good middle path at Series A is often a senior fractional consultant plus a boutique agency or launch sprint for a concentrated moment, then reassessing after 3-6 months. [chip: shilikajain +1]

Practical decision rule (table)
Situation | Better fit
Pre-Series B, story still unclear, founder available | Fractional consultant [chip: shilikajain +1]
One launch or funding announcement in 60-90 days | Fractional consultant or launch sprint [chip: shilikajain +1]
Series A with defined narrative and steady news | Fractional or boutique agency [chip: shilikajain]
Multi-market launch, analyst program, crisis coverage | Agency, ideally with in-house comms lead [chip: shilikajain +1]
No product-market fit, no spokesperson, no news hook | Delay paid PR; focus on founder-led content and customer development [chip: firebrand]

How to vet either option
Ask for the last three relevant placements they personally drove, the angle used, and a reference from a comparable AI company. Require a written scope that separates strategy, pitching, content, wire costs, and KOL/influencer fees; avoid engagements that promise coverage rather than a clear process, target outlets, and measurable milestones. [chip: shilikajain +1]

Bottom line: start fractional if you need to sharpen the story and build early credibility; move to an agency when the work has outgrown one senior operator and you need sustained execution infrastructure. [chip: shilikajain +1]
