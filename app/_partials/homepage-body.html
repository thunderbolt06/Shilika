What does a fractional PR manager do for a Web3 or AI startup?

A fractional PR manager runs the full PR function on a part-time retainer instead of an agency contract. For Web3 and AI founders, that means one senior operator handling narrative, embargoed press, founder profiling, KOL waves, APAC localisation, and launch comms. The retainer is sized to the work, founder access is direct, and accountability is tied to placements in Forbes, CoinDesk, Cointelegraph, Decrypt, The Block and Blockworks rather than billable hours.

How much does fractional PR for a Web3 or AI startup cost in 2026?

Fractional PR retainers run a fraction of a traditional agency contract because there are no account-team layers. Web3 retainers typically sit at $5K to $14K per month, AI startup retainers at $6K to $14K, cybersecurity retainers at $7.5K to $15K, and token launch sprints at $20K to $60K. Full 2026 pricing teardown is here. Book a 30-minute teardown for a scoped quote.

How do I get my Web3 or AI startup featured in CoinDesk or Cointelegraph?

Lead with a category-level story, not a funding or launch announcement. Map the right reporter on the desk that covers your space, write an under-180-word pitch with a dated news peg, and offer a real embargo confirmed in writing 7 to 10 days out. The two newsrooms work differently: two step-by-step operator guides cover each. How to get featured in CoinDesk in 2026 and How to pitch Cointelegraph in 2026.

How is fractional PR different from a Web3 PR agency?

A Web3 PR agency assigns a junior account team, starts retainers higher, and filters founder access through layers. Fractional PR is one senior operator embedded with the founder, no markup on placements, and accountability tied to coverage in named outlets rather than billable hours. The full side-by-side is in Fractional PR vs Web3 PR agency: how to choose in 2026.
