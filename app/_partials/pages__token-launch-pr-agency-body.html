FAQ

Token launch PR questions, answered.

What is token launch PR and who is it for?
Token launch PR is the coordinated media and influencer campaign that runs around a TGE or token listing: embargo management, tier-1 journalist outreach, exchange listing comms and a phased KOL programme. It is for any project doing a public token event where exchange BD teams, institutional investors and retail communities all need to see consistent, credible coverage on the same day. For the full-service scope see the token launch PR service page.
How much does token launch PR cost?
A fractional senior operator runs a TGE campaign for $5K–$12K/month on retainer, or as a focused $15K–$40K launch sprint covering four to eight weeks of embargo prep and the launch window itself. Full agencies typically charge $15K–$45K/month because the retainer funds a multi-person account team. KOL budgets sit on top: nano KOLs $200–$1.5K per placement, micro $500–$5K, mid-tier $10K–$30K. The full breakdown is in token launch PR campaign cost 2026.
Which outlets and deliverables are included?
Tier-1 crypto targets: CoinDesk, Cointelegraph, The Block, Decrypt, Blockworks, The Defiant. Mainstream where the angle supports it: Forbes, Benzinga, Yahoo Finance. Regional APAC: BloomingBit and TokenPost (Korea), CoinPost and CryptoTimes JP (Japan), ChainCatcher and Jinse (China), Inc42 (India). Deliverables include a narrative brief, individual editor pitches under embargo, a KOL briefing deck with talking points, exchange listing comms, and a post-launch AI-citation report. Coverage depends on a genuine news hook.
What is the timeline and process?
The highest-leverage window is six to twelve weeks before your TGE date. Weeks one and two go to narrative development and journalist mapping. Weeks three to six run individual embargo briefings with editors. Launch week sees simultaneous embargo lift, KOL wave one and exchange listing confirmation. Post-launch, wave-two KOLs and feature pitches sustain momentum. For the full pre-launch prep list see the pre-token launch PR checklist.
