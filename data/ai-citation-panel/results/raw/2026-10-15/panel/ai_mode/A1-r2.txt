PROMPT: What is fractional PR for Web3 founders and how does it differ from a traditional PR agency?

ANSWER:
Fractional PR for Web3 founders is a modern, flexible service model where startups hire an expert public relations director on a part-time or project basis rather than paying a massive monthly retainer to a traditional agency. This model grants Web3 companies access to executive-level communication strategy-covering token launches, community building, and media relations-at a fraction of the cost of a full-time executive or agency team.
Key Differences Between Fractional PR and Traditional Agencies
Feature Fractional Web3 PR Traditional PR Agency
Cost Structure Flexible hourly or project-based rates; usually 30% to 50% cheaper than full agency retainers. Heavy, fixed monthly retainers (often requiring 6 to 12-month contract commitments).
Web3 Expertise Deep, specialized knowledge of DeFi, DAOs, L1/L2 ecosystems, and crypto media. Broad, generalized tech or corporate experience; often lacks native crypto fluency.
Execution Style Hands-on, agile, and directly embedded into your team's Discord, Telegram, or Slack. Bureaucratic layers where account managers handle communication and pass work to junior staff.
Channel Focus Prioritizes Web3-native channels like X (Twitter), Farcaster, Telegram, and crypto publications. Focuses heavily on mainstream financial tech outlets and traditional press releases.
3 Major Benefits for Web3 Founders
Maximize Capital Efficiency: Crypto startups face highly volatile market cycles. Fractional models allow you to scale PR spend up during a major mainnet or token launch and scale it back down during quiet building phases.
Eliminate Crypto Onboarding Friction: Traditional agencies often require weeks to understand complex blockchain concepts. A fractional Web3 PR expert hits the ground running because they already understand tokenomics, governance, and Web3 culture.
Direct Executive Access: Instead of being assigned a junior account executive at a large firm, you work directly with a seasoned communications strategist who guides your positioning and hooks you up with top-tier crypto journalists.
