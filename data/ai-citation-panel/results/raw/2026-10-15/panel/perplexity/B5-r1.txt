Run it as a performance-marketing program, not a one-off "shoutout" buy: define an on-chain outcome, vet creators for audience quality and credibility, activate in coordinated waves around a product catalyst, and attribute wallets - not clicks - to each creator. 1. Set one primary outcome

Choose a single primary KPI tied to the protocol stage: funded wallets, first deposits, TVL, active users, governance participation, or 30-day retained wallets. Reach and impressions should remain secondary.

Prioritize relevance and trust over follower count. A KOL should already speak credibly to your ideal user - DeFi traders, developers, gamers, or infrastructure builders - rather than merely having a large crypto audience.

3. Design a three-wave campaign

Time the campaign around a real catalyst: testnet, mainnet, token launch, listing, major feature, or governance milestone.
wisebusinessplans

Wave | Timing | Purpose | Content
Whisper / credibility | 2-3 days to weeks before launch | Establish the narrative with trusted specialists | Deep-dive threads, technical explainers, early access
Tease / social proof | 1 week before launch | Expand awareness and build anticipation | Comparisons, AMAs, previews, mid-tier posts
Shout / activation | Launch window | Convert attention into wallet actions | Tutorials, YouTube reviews, X threads, Telegram posts
Sustain | 24 hours to 60-90 days after | Retain users and reinforce the story | Long-form analyses, podcasts, product updates, AMAs

This sequencing prevents the launch from reading as a purely paid promotion: credible technical voices frame the product first, then broader creators amplify it.

5. Instrument on-chain attribution before launch

Set up tracking before the first post. Assign each KOL a unique UTM-tagged landing page or referral path; persist the source data through wallet connection; then monitor downstream on-chain activity.

First transactions, deposits, swaps, mints, or other protocol actions.

7-, 30-, and 60-day wallet retention.

TVL, volume, or holder retention from each creator cohort.

6. Support traffic with community

KOL traffic converts better when it lands in an active Discord or Telegram, with moderators who can answer technical questions and regular AMAs with the core team. Avoid inflated member counts; serious users notice.

Repurpose high-performing long-form collaborations - podcasts, interviews, AMAs - into 30-60 second clips for X, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and other channels over the following weeks.

7. Optimize in waves

Run each wave for roughly two to four weeks, then review performance. Scale creators whose cost per activation beats target, re-brief or replace weak performers, and reallocate budget toward the best-performing regions and formats.
