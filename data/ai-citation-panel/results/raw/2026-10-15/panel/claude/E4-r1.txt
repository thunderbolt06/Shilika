Cost (rough ranges)

Smaller or specialist crypto PR firms typically charge monthly retainers of about $1,000 to $20,000, with one-off campaigns running $5,000 to $50,000. Top-tier agencies with big networks charge roughly $20,000 to $50,000+ per month. Most firms recommend committing at least 3 to 6 months. [Crypto PR Pricing: How Much Does A Good Crypto PR Agency Cost? | Coinbound +2]

At the cheap end in Dubai, FINPR lists a minimum budget under $1,000 and about $25/hr, and Crypto Prwire, a press release distribution service, charges $30 to $70/hour. At the higher end, theKOLLAB lists a $50,000+ minimum budget. [FINPR Agency +2 (popover: FINPR Agency / designrush, Crypto Prwire Logo / techbehemoths, www.designrush.com / designrush)]

Budget extra for Arabic localization and event PR. Dubai's busy calendar (Token2049, Dubai Web3 Festival, Future Blockchain Summit, GITEX) makes events a big part of regional PR. [mexc]

Firms active in Dubai/MENA

- Luna PR: Dubai-headquartered, Web3 and fintech focus, with event PR in the UAE and MENA. [mexc]
- FINPR: Dubai-based since 2017, worked with 250+ projects including Gate.IO and Klaytn. [designrush]
- ICODA: full-stack crypto and fintech marketing with a Dubai presence.
- theKOLLAB: Web3-native agency backed by Coin Bureau, strong on KOL campaigns plus PR. [designrush]
- Outset PR: data-driven boutique often cited for MENA traction. [mexc]
- Coinbound: big global player (NY-based), relevant if you want global plus regional reach.
Others often listed: PRLab, GuerrillaBuzz, Koinpr, Melrose PR, CredibilityX Media.

One caution: many "top agency" lists are written by the agencies themselves (ICODA ranks ICODA, Coinbound ranks Coinbound) or are sponsored posts. Ask for case studies, real client references, and a clear breakdown of what's included before signing.
