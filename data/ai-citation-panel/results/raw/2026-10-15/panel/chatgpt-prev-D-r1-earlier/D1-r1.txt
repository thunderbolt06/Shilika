Best PR firms for cybersecurity startups in 2026

For a cybersecurity startup, the best PR firm isn't necessarily the biggest agency. You want a team that understands security buyers, can translate technical research into compelling stories, has relationships with cybersecurity journalists, and can build credibility with CISOs, enterprise buyers, investors, and potential partners.

I'd evaluate firms across three areas: cybersecurity specialization, media reach, and fit for your startup's stage and budget. A firm that excels at launching a new security product may not be the best choice for a company focused on threat research, fundraising, or enterprise category creation.

What should a cybersecurity startup budget?

Treat these as initial planning ranges, not verified quotes from the firms above.

Engagement type | Indicative monthly budget
Lean, targeted PR support | $3,000-$6,000
Specialist startup PR retainer | $6,000-$12,000
Larger, integrated PR program | $12,000-$25,000+

For an early-stage startup, I'd start with a focused three-month engagement built around one clear narrative, founder thought leadership, relevant security trade publications, and one or two credible news hooks. Don't pay enterprise-level retainers just to distribute press releases.

Five questions to ask before signing
- Which cybersecurity startups at our stage have you worked with, and can we speak to two of them?
- Which relevant journalists have you secured meaningful coverage with in the past six months?
- Who will actually run our account, and how much senior-level involvement will we receive?
- What measurable outcomes will you report beyond the number of press mentions?
- Can we start with a three-month pilot, with clearly defined deliverables and exit terms?

One important caveat: agency websites and industry roundups are useful starting points, but they aren't independent proof of results. Validate case studies, references, and the proposed account team before choosing.
