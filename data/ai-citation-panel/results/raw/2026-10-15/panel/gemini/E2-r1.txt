3. What Makes Japanese Crypto PR Unique?

X (Twitter) and Local Media Dominance: Japanese crypto audiences rely heavily on X (Twitter) and dedicated native publications rather than Discord, Telegram, or Western outlets. Effective PR agencies combine press releases with localized X campaigns.

Translation & Localization is Mandatory: Direct English-to-Japanese press release translations perform poorly. Successful PR teams re-architect narratives to fit Japanese business etiquette and regulatory expectations.

FSA Regulatory Sensitivity: Local outlets will not publish campaigns promoting unregistered exchanges or dubious token sales. Japanese PR firms ensure messaging complies with FSA rules.
