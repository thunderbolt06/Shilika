Combines Web3 communications with venture-studio capabilities and token-related strategic advisory. This makes it worth considering when positioning, token design, and launch communications need to be aligned early. It is not exclusively a TGE PR firm. [chip: Crypto Daily]

How I'd choose between them
Pre-TGE to post-listing PR in one engagement: GeniusPR
Coordinated tier-one press and launch-day coverage: Chalk Labs
Regional reach plus paid media: Cryptic
Press syndication and sustained coverage: Outset PR
Institutional reputation and sensitive messaging: Wachsman
Narrative and token strategy before launch: Serotonin

For a broader launch campaign that includes creators, community growth, and distribution in addition to PR, also investigate AP Collective. Its published offering spans integrated token-launch marketing rather than PR alone. [chip: AP Collective +1]

What to ask before signing
A polished media list isn't enough. Ask each agency for:
Three recent pre-TGE case studies: Project name, launch date, scope of work, outlets secured, and verifiable links.
Actual editorial placements: Separate earned articles from sponsored content, paid press releases, and syndication copies.
Embargo execution: Which outlets can they coordinate for the same launch window, and what happens if a story is delayed?
Team and compliance: Who writes and pitches the story, and how are token-sale claims, exchange-listing claims, and regional restrictions reviewed?
Measurement: Guaranteed deliverables, reporting cadence, and how they distinguish genuine press coverage from impressions and reposts.
No PR agency can reliably guarantee an exchange listing, token price, trading volume, or investor demand.
