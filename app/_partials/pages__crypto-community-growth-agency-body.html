Crypto community growth questions, answered.

What is a crypto community growth agency and who is it for?
A crypto community growth agency coordinates KOL activations, Telegram and Discord content, and media placements so they reinforce each other rather than running as separate, unconnected sprints. It is for Web3 and AI founders who have a real product but whose community is not growing between announcement cycles. Shilika Jain runs this as a fractional operator for $5K–$12K/month, covering KOL strategy, content and PR under one narrative. For the KOL-specific scope see the KOL and influencer marketing service page.
How much does crypto community growth cost?
A fractional senior operator running KOL, content and PR together costs $5K–$12K/month, compared to $15K–$45K/month for a traditional agency that staffs a separate account team. KOL budgets sit on top: nano creators run $200–$1.5K per activation, micro creators $500–$5K, and mid-tier creators $10K–$30K. A focused launch-sprint engagement runs $15K–$40K over four to eight weeks and is scoped at the brief stage.
Which KOL tiers and platforms do you work with?
The programme covers nano creators (under 10K followers, $200–$1.5K), micro creators (10K–100K, $500–$5K) and mid-tier creators (100K–500K, $10K–$30K) across X, YouTube, Telegram and regional platforms in Korea, Japan, Vietnam and India. Macro creators ($25K–$100K+) are available for major launches. Creator selection is based on audience quality, not raw follower count, and every creator is briefed on the same narrative anchor rather than left to improvise.
How long does it take to see community growth results?
A coordinated KOL wave tied to a media placement typically produces measurable community growth within two to four weeks of activation. Sustained month-on-month growth that holds between announcements requires a three-to-six month programme where content, KOL cadence and PR are kept in rhythm. One-off activations produce one-off spikes. The programme is designed for the former.
