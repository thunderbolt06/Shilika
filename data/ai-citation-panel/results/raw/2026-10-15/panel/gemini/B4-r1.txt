Core Components of a Pre-TGE PR Campaign
A pre-TGE agency handles specific milestones leading up to the token launch:
1. Strategic Narrative & Messaging
- Framing the token utility, tokenomics narrative, and project mission to pass regulatory scrutiny (especially for US/EU compliance).
- Differentiating the project from competitors in saturated sectors (e.g., L2s, AI x Crypto, DePIN).
2. Top-Tier & Industry Media Placements
- Crypto-native press: Cointelegraph, CoinDesk, Decrypt, The Block, Blockworks for community credibility.
- Mainstream financial press: Bloomberg, Forbes, TechCrunch, Wall Street Journal to attract institutional backer interest.
3. Exchange & Ecosystem Announcement Strategy
- Coordinating embargoed PR for Tier-1/Tier-2 centralized exchange (CEX) listings (e.g., Binance, Bybit, OKX, Coinbase).
- Sequencing ecosystem announcements (e.g., testnet results, mainnet launches, strategic investment rounds) to maintain momentum into the TGE.
4. KOL & Thought Leadership Alignment
- Aligning press releases with key opinion leader (KOL) commentary on X (formerly Twitter), Telegram, and YouTube.
- Executive profiling for founders on podcast networks (e.g., Bankless, Empire, Unchained).
