How I would choose

Pre-seed or Seed: Start with a specialist that will give you senior attention and can build a compelling founder and product narrative.

Series A: Prioritize repeatable media outreach, category positioning, customer proof points, and launch campaigns.

Series B and beyond: Look for integrated PR, executive thought leadership, analyst relations, and international reach.

AI security or threat research: Ask for evidence of successful technical research campaigns and experience explaining vulnerabilities responsibly.

Breach or crisis communications: Make sure the agency has a real incident-response communications playbook, not just ordinary media relations.

Before signing, ask each finalist for two relevant cybersecurity case studies, the names and experience of the actual account team, sample coverage in publications such as Dark Reading and The Record, and three-month success metrics. Treat guaranteed media coverage claims carefully.
