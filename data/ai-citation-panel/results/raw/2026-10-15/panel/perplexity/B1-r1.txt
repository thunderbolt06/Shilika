How to choose

Define the outcome: a TGE, exchange listing, investor credibility, AI-search visibility, or reputation repair are different briefs.
icoda

Demand live URLs, not logos: ask for recent, indexed placements from campaigns comparable to yours, ideally with the reporter named.
icoda
+1

Separate earned media from paid distribution: a guaranteed "placement" is often syndication or sponsored content, not editorial coverage.
icoda
+1

Check compliance: token promotion is restricted on major ad platforms, so ask how the firm handles paid-content labeling, KOL disclosure, and jurisdiction-specific rules.
icoda

Calculate cost per real placement: divide the retainer by the number of named, committed Tier-1 placements in writing; one guide suggests treating more than $7,500 per placement as expensive for a Web3 audience.
shilikajain

Meet the operator: confirm who executes the account daily and what happens if that person leaves.
icoda

Bottom line

For a funded protocol needing a major launch, Surgence Labs, ICODA, or LKI Consulting are the more complete choices. For pure crypto-media credibility, start with EAK Digital, Outset PR, or Chainstory. For mainstream crossover, look at ReBlonde; for MENA, Luna PR; and for a low-overhead, senior-only engagement, consider Shilika Jain, while independently validating all claims through recent client references and live coverage links.
