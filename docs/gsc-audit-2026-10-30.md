# Search Console audit: shilikajain.com, 30 Oct 2026

Source: Search Console API (`sc-domain:shilikajain.com`, service account, read-only), pulled 30 Oct 2026. Data runs from 12 May 2026, when the property first shows impressions. Period comparisons use **finalized** data only: **L28 = 1-28 Oct**, **P28 = 3-30 Sep**. URL Inspection was run on all 821 sitemap URLs, plus `/article-5` and the non-www root (823 in total).

Context: the coverage remediation in `docs/gsc-recrawl-2026-10-30.md` (commit `bf80c18`) reached production on the evening of 30 Oct. Nothing in this audit reflects it yet. Treat this report as the "before" baseline.

## 1. Summary

1. **Traffic is growing from a very small base.** Clicks 33 → 64 (+94%), impressions 9,029 → 10,563 (+17%). Clicks to pages other than the homepage doubled (22 → 46). In total the site has had 183 clicks and ~45k impressions in 5.5 months.
2. **Rankings improved like for like.** For the 47 query+page pairs present in both periods, average position went 15.3 → 13.3 (26 improved, 10 worsened). The site-wide average got worse (12.1 → 16.4), but that's a mix effect. About 270 pages new in L28 (excluding `/tools`) average position 9.4. The drag comes from two places: the 28 new `/tools/*` pages (~1,130 impressions at ~#42), and existing pages that started appearing for head terms at #60-80 ("crypto pr", "cybersecurity pr"). Those head-term impressions took carried-over pages from 12.3 to 17.0.
3. **Clicks lag visibility, but part of the visibility isn't real demand.** Site CTR is 0.61%. 240 attributed queries ranked at positions 4-10 in L28 and produced 1 click between them, and pages other than the homepage converted at 0.45%. The device split separates real gaps from noise:
   - `/playbook/best-defi-pr-agency-2026` gets 43% of its impressions from mobile at #6-9 and still has 0 clicks. That is a real snippet or intent problem.
   - The Cointelegraph guide (627 of 629 impressions on desktop) and the B2B SaaS list (228 of 240) are almost all desktop, which points to trackers or agents, not people skipping the result.
4. **A large share of impressions doesn't look human.** Desktop takes 88% of impressions at 0.54% CTR, against 1.0% on mobile. In L28, 19 operator or scraper queries (`"@gmail.com"`, `-site:`, `after:`, hashes, prompt-style `<brand>… respond in english`) made 834 impressions, 16% of attributed impressions. Weekly unique queries tripled from the week of 28 Sep. Treat raw impression growth with caution.
5. **About half of impressions and three-quarters of clicks are anonymized** (Google withholds rare queries). Query-level brand and non-brand splits therefore undercount. Use page-level data for click analysis.
6. **Several pages rank for the wrong intent.** The geo hubs (`/india`, `/apac`, `/japan`, `/korea`, `/singapore`) and `/tools/*` take more than 2,000 impressions a month at positions 20-65 with zero clicks. They show for market-research queries ("india web3 in retail market") and generic tool queries ("headline checker"), not for buyers of PR.
7. **Indexing: 623 of 821 sitemap URLs (76%) are indexed.** The other 198 are:
   - 118 unknown to Google. 102 of them are blog posts published 28-29 Oct, after Google last read the sitemap on 24 Oct.
   - 42 "Crawled, not indexed", a quality verdict. It includes pages that used to earn impressions (2,116 all-time, led by `/playbook/ai-thought-leadership-pr-2026` with 455).
   - 37 "Discovered, not indexed", mostly locale pages and `/compare/*`.
   - 1 page Google got a 4xx on (`/pages/crypto-content-marketing-agency`).
8. **October publishing was heavy, and so far Google has accepted it.** 321 of the 402 blog posts were published in October, in three batches: 100 on 1 Oct, 119 on 15 Oct and 102 on 28-29 Oct (dates from each post's `datePublished`). All 219 posts from the first two batches are indexed. Every blog post marked "crawled, not indexed" is from May or June. The quality verdicts sit on older `/playbook`, `/pages` and early blog content, not on the new batches. The risk to watch is Google's site-level quality judgement as volume keeps scaling, so check that the 28 Oct batch indexes as cleanly before publishing more.

Sections 2-4 cover performance. Section 5 covers indexing, section 6 structured data, and section 7 the action list.

### What moved, and why

- **Cointelegraph guide (−1,950 impressions): demand change, not a demotion.** Nearly all of its impressions came from one query, "cointelegraph submit press release". That query's weekly impressions went ~300-950 (Sep) → 60 (mid-Oct) → 6, while its position *improved* from 9 to 5. In L28, 627 of its 629 impressions were desktop. This looks like a rank tracker or agent that stopped, not human demand. In 5.5 months the page has 3,790 impressions and 8 clicks.
- **`/playbook/crypto-pr-cost-2026` (avg pos 8 → 40): mix, plus a lost long-tail.** In September it held #2 for "how much does professional crypto pr cost monthly?" and "what's the typical cost for blockchain pr services?". Those queries are gone in October. Meanwhile it started showing for "crypto pr" (#78), "cryptocurrency pr" (#67) and "crypto pr packages" (#75), which drags the average down. "crypto pr pricing" held at ~#11. It also shares "crypto pr" with `/playbook/crypto-pr-vs-ai-pr` (#84).

### Sitemap

- Google last downloaded the sitemap on 24 Oct (719 URLs). The live sitemap has 821, and it was last submitted on 2 Jul.
- `lastmod` is honest for static routes (`app/sitemap.ts` sets it only on real edits). Blog posts take it from Supabase `updated_at`, though, and bulk patches move it for everything they touch. 103 posts show lastmod 25 Oct and 66 show 29 Oct, including June posts and the 15 Oct batch. Those dates line up with the routine runs 95-102 patches (em-dash sweep and others), and the `trg_set_updated_at` trigger always overwrites `updated_at`. If dozens of URLs get a new lastmod for cosmetic edits, Google learns to discount lastmod site-wide. Consider a separate `content_updated_at` column that only moves on substantive edits, and feed the sitemap from that.

**Fixed 30 Oct (same branch):**
- The conference `mentions` in articles 47, 71, 84, 90 and 116 are now `Thing`. That covers the 4 flagged pages plus `/playbook/crypto-kol-marketing-korea-2026`, which had a dated Event with no location.

## Not covered by the API (check in the GSC UI)

The Search Console API doesn't expose these, so this audit doesn't cover them:
- **Manual actions** and **Security issues**: check both once (Security & Manual Actions in the left nav). With 300+ posts published in a month, a scaled-content manual action is the one to rule out.
- **Links** (top linking sites, internal link counts).
- **Core Web Vitals** and HTTPS reports.
- **Page Indexing report history** (the trend of indexed vs not-indexed over time). URL Inspection gives today's state only.
