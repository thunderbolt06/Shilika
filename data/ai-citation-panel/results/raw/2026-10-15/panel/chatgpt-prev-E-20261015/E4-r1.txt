4. What I'd ask agencies before signing

A low monthly fee can be misleading if the actual media placements, local expertise or creator costs are not included.

Regional proof: Which Dubai or GCC campaigns have you personally run? Can you share published examples?
Earned vs. paid: What proportion of expected coverage is editorial versus sponsored or syndicated?
Deliverables: How many pitches, interviews, founder profiles and substantive placements are targeted each month?
Local execution: Who handles UAE journalist relationships, Arabic-language outreach and regional event coordination?
Exclusions: Are newswire fees, KOLs, event access, travel and localization included?
Reporting: Will you provide live links, publication quality, audience relevance and referral traffic-not just impressions?
Commercial terms: Is there a minimum term, cancellation clause, setup fee or additional fee for a launch sprint?

I'd avoid signing a long retainer based on promised publication names alone. Ask for a 90-day plan with measurable deliverables and a clear distinction between guaranteed paid placements and earned-media targets.

For an institutional or regulated business: Prioritize regional business press, accurate regulatory messaging and credibility over raw crypto-media placement volume.
