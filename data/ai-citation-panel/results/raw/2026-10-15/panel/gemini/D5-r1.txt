Top Specialized Cybersecurity PR Agencies
1. Crackle PR (Best for Senior-Led CISO & Threat-Intel PR)

Best for: Seed to Series B startups that need immediate credibility with CISOs, threat intelligence validation, and AI/search discoverability.

Why them: Founded by Parry Headrick, Crackle is a senior-only boutique known for running aggressive, zero-fluff campaigns. They specialize in translating complex security architectures into mainstream enterprise media narratives and handle breach responses/crisis comms. [chip: Crackle PR + 1]

Typical Retainer: Starting around $12,000/month. [chip: Crackle PR]

2. Highwire PR (Best for Scale-ups & Enterprise Positioning)

Best for: Series A+ startups looking for deep US enterprise reach.

Why them: Highwire maintains one of the largest dedicated cybersecurity practices in the US. They are exceptional at positioning non-US founders in major tier-1 business press (Wall Street Journal, TechCrunch, Forbes) and cybersecurity trade outlets (Dark Reading, SC Media).

Typical Retainer: ~$20,000+/month. [chip: Crackle PR]

3. Lumina Communications (Best Boutique for Deep Tech & Infrastructure)

Best for: Technical security products (e.g., cloud security, IAM, devsecops, identity) needing specialized narrative development.

Why them: Silicon Valley and NYC-based boutique that specializes heavily in cybersecurity and infrastructure. They are great at bridging the gap between foreign tech leadership and the US venture/media ecosystem. [chip: Crackle PR]

4. 10Fold Communications (Best for US Regional & Silicon Valley Roots)

Best for: High-growth tech startups aiming for strong Silicon Valley presence and analyst relations (Gartner, Forrester).

Why them: Strong track record helping international B2B tech startups navigate US market entries, combining media relations, thought leadership, and digital PR. [chip: PRLab]

5. PRLab (Best for European Startups Crossing the Atlantic)

Best for: EU/UK-based cybersecurity startups needing a dual bridge between European headquarters and US expansion.

Why them: They run specialized US expansion programs out of their Austin office and focus on tying PR directly to pipeline, investor interest, and SEO/AI search visibility.

Top Independent PR Consultants & Advisory

If you are an early-stage startup (pre-Series A) without the $12k-$20k/month retainer required for full-service agencies, an independent consultant or fractional VP of Communications is often a better route:

Parry Headrick (Crackle PR / Advisory): Frequently acts as a direct advisor for cybersecurity founders on market positioning before ramping up full agency retainers.

Ray George (Solo Consultant / Security PR Specialist): A veteran independent security PR practitioner with decades of experience launching overseas security startups into the US.

Boutique Counsel via VC Networks: If you have US or international VC backing (e.g., Y Combinator, Cyberstarts, Glilot Capital, Bessemer), reach out to their internal communications partners. They often maintain vetted lists of solo PR consultants who charge $5,000-$8,000/month for project-based launches.

Key Criteria for Choosing a US Cybersecurity PR Partner

When evaluating candidates for US market expansion, ensure they meet these specific security PR needs:

Threat Research Amplification: Can they take your internal threat research or vulnerability reports and turn them into headline news in outlets like Dark Reading, BleepingComputer, or KrebsonSecurity?

Analyst Relations (AR): Do they have direct connections with US-based Gartner, Forrester, and IDC security analysts?

CISO Fluency: Do they understand the language of enterprise CISOs, or are they pitching generic B2B tech jargon that security journalists filter out?

GEO / AI Visibility: Do they optimize PR placements so your brand is cited when buyers research vendors via AI platforms (e.g., ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini)? [chip: Crackle PR]
