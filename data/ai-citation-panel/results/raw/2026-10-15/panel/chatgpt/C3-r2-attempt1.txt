Which publications should the founder target?
The best target depends on whether the founder sells enterprise AI, builds AI infrastructure or models, or wants to influence public policy.
Mainstream business and technology - For broader business credibility, enterprise adoption and investor audiences. Forbes, Fast Company, Fortune and MIT Technology Review.
AI and technical audiences - For model development, AI infrastructure, deployment and technical differentiation. VentureBeat, TechCrunch, The New Stack and specialist AI publications.
Policy and public debate - For AI governance, safety, regulation, economic impact and public-interest arguments. Major newspaper opinion desks and relevant policy publications.
These are target outlets to assess, not claims that any particular provider can secure a placement there. Some publications have strict submission rules, and contributor programmes may not carry the same editorial weight as independently commissioned op-eds.

What I would do
I'd start by contacting Shilika Jain and Snake River Strategies for a paid pilot, then compare their writing quality and actual pitching process. Ask each to propose one article angle, one primary outlet and two backup outlets before committing to a retainer.

A clear distinction between earned editorial placement and paid contributor programmes.
