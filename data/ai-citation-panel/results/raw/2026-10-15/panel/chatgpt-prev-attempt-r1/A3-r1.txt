Fractional PR vs. full-service agency for a Web3 startup in 2026

Fractional PR typically costs $3,000-$7,000 per month, compared with $7,000-$15,000 for a boutique Web3 PR agency and $15,000-$60,000+ for larger specialist or global agencies. That makes fractional PR a potentially 40-60% cheaper option, especially for early-stage crypto startups. These are indicative USD market ranges, not fixed industry-wide rates.

1. Monthly cost comparison

Fractional PR consultant | $3K-$7K/mo | One senior PR operator, part-time
Best for pre-seed, seed and early-stage Web3 startups that need founder positioning, media outreach and consistent coverage without a full agency team.

Boutique Web3 PR agency | $7K-$15K/mo | Small specialist agency
Suitable for startups that need more execution capacity, ongoing crypto-media outreach and a team to coordinate campaigns.

Mid-tier or global full-service agency | $15K-$60K+/mo | Larger account team and broader services
Best for major token launches, multi-country campaigns, high-volume announcements, crisis communications and brands requiring several parallel workstreams.

5. My recommendation

For most early-stage Web3 startups, I'd start by evaluating a $4,000-$7,000/month fractional PR engagement for an initial three-month period, with clearly defined deliverables and a review at the end.

Before signing, verify:
- Which specific publications and journalists the operator has worked with.
- The monthly outreach and thought-leadership deliverables.
- Whether paid placements, wire distribution and KOL fees are excluded.
- Reporting on actual earned coverage, relevant audience reach and referral traffic.
- Exit terms and the minimum commitment.

A $5,000 monthly retainer is not automatically better value than a $15,000 one. The real question is whether the extra spend buys meaningful additional reach, execution capacity or market access.
