A useful distinction: the market includes independent fractional consultants as well as agencies. They aren't interchangeable. A consultant may give you direct access to a senior operator; an agency may offer more execution capacity, regional coverage, and crisis support.

Five questions to ask before signing
- Can you show me three earned editorial stories you personally secured in the last 12 months for startups like mine?
- Which journalists will you pitch, and who will actually do the pitching?
- How do you distinguish independent editorial coverage from sponsored articles and press-release syndication?
- What are the deliverables for the first 30 and 90 days, and how will success be measured?
- Have you handled our particular category, such as DeFi, stablecoins, DePIN, infrastructure or a token launch?

Be cautious about anyone guaranteeing coverage in prestigious editorial outlets. Paid publication and independently earned journalism are different products.
