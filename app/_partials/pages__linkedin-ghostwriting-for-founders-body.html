It is a done-for-you content service where a senior operator interviews you, extracts the perspectives and experience you already have, and turns them into a weekly publishing cadence on LinkedIn. How much does LinkedIn ghostwriting for founders cost?
A full ghostwriting retainer, covering strategy, 3-4 posts per week, editing and monthly review, runs $5K–$12K per month depending on posting frequency and whether op-ed placement is included. That is a senior operator rate with no junior writers involved. Generic content mills charge $500–$2K per month and the output reads accordingly.
What does the content cover and what are the deliverables?
Deliverables are 12–20 posts per month, structured around four to six pillars specific to your sector and business goals: category commentary, behind-the-build transparency, industry contrarian takes, and company milestones framed as insight rather than announcements. Op-ed placement in CoinDesk, Forbes, Decrypt or Cointelegraph can be added as a separate layer on the same retainer.
How long before I see results?
LinkedIn reach compounds slowly for the first four to six weeks while the algorithm calibrates to your new posting pattern. Most founders see a meaningful shift in inbound quality, investor and journalist DMs, unsolicited connection requests from relevant people, within 60 to 90 days of consistent, high-quality output. The goal is not a viral post on week one but a position that holds for three years.
