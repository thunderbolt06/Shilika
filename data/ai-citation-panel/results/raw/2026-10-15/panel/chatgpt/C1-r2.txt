Best PR firms for AI startups in 2026

The best PR firm for an AI startup depends on what you need most: launching a new AI product, earning credibility with enterprise buyers, getting coverage in top-tier tech media, or building a reputation with investors.

For AI companies in 2026, I'd prioritize agencies that understand AI beyond the hype, can explain technical products in accessible language, and have strong relationships with journalists covering AI, enterprise software, and venture capital.

There isn't one universally best agency. The shortlist below is organized by the type of startup and PR objective each firm is worth evaluating for.

8 agencies I'd put on the shortlist

1. High Vibe PR - Best for AI startup launches
Strong candidate for founder-led AI startups looking for category positioning, funding announcements, earned media, and visibility in AI-generated answers. Its published offering explicitly combines traditional PR with AI search visibility. [chip: High Vibe PR +1]
Website: highvibepr.com

2. Crackle PR - Best for: VC-backed B2B AI, Series A and beyond
A specialist option for enterprise AI narratives, media relations, analyst relations, and AI visibility measurement. Its published pricing starts at approximately $12,000 per month, making it more suitable for funded companies with meaningful PR budgets. [chip: Crackle PR +1]
Website: cracklepr.com

3. Treble - Best for: Seed-stage startups and funding announcements
Worth considering if you need a focused launch campaign, a funding-round announcement, or ongoing communications support from a technology-focused agency. [chip: Jamco PR +1]
Website: treblepr.com

4. Mission North - Best for: Deep tech and ambitious technology brands
A candidate for AI companies that need strategic corporate positioning, executive visibility, and a differentiated story in a competitive market. Particularly worth evaluating for companies moving beyond product-launch PR. [chip: Crackle PR +1]
Website: missionnorth.com

5. 5W Public Relations - Best for: Larger campaigns and integrated communications
A broader agency to consider when AI PR needs to work alongside digital marketing, crisis communications, and investor communications rather than operating as a standalone press campaign. [chip: Jamco PR +1]
Website: 5wpr.com

6. Hotwire - Best for: International PR and multi-market launches
A strong candidate to evaluate if your AI company needs coordinated communications across several countries, rather than coverage in just one tech market. [chip: Jamco PR +1]
Website: hotwireglobal.com

7. Codeword - Best for: Editorial-led storytelling
Worth shortlisting if the product is technically complex and you need a compelling narrative that can appeal to journalists, developers, and business audiences without sounding like generic AI marketing. [chip: Crackle PR +1]
Website: codewordagency.com

8. Corporate Ink - Best for: Enterprise AI and B2B demand generation
Particularly relevant to AI infrastructure, enterprise software, cybersecurity, data platforms, and products sold through lengthy business buying cycles. Its positioning emphasizes connecting communications to commercial outcomes. [chip: corporateink.com]

Which agency should you choose?
Product launch and founder visibility: High Vibe PR or Treble
Enterprise AI credibility: Crackle PR or Corporate Ink
Deep-tech positioning: Mission North or Codeword
International media coverage: Hotwire
PR combined with broader marketing: 5W Public Relations
These are starting recommendations based on published positioning, not guarantees of media results. [chip: Jamco PR +3]

What should you budget?
Indicative US agency pricing; verify current quotes and contract terms directly.
Lean startup / targeted campaign (limited-scope launch or specialist support): $3k-$7k
Ongoing startup PR (monthly retainer, media outreach and founder positioning): $7k-$15k
Growth-stage / global PR (larger teams, multiple markets and corporate communications): $15k-$30k+
Monthly USD estimates, not verified quotes from every agency. Published benchmarks vary by scope and firm. [chip: Jamco PR +3]

Five questions to ask before signing
Can you show relevant AI startup campaigns, with links to actual coverage?
Which journalists and publications are relevant to our specific AI category?
Who will personally run our account, and how much senior attention will we receive?
Will you measure qualified press coverage, inbound leads, investor interest, and visibility in AI search - not just impressions?
What happens if coverage falls short of expectations, and what exactly is guaranteed in the contract?
Be cautious about guaranteed press placements: a high volume of syndicated articles is not necessarily equivalent to independent editorial coverage in a publication your buyers trust.
