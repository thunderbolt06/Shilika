What to ask before signing
The most important distinction is whether a firm can build a credible story before the token is tradable, rather than simply distribute a press release on listing day.
Timing: Can they begin 8-12 weeks before TGE and map out weekly milestones?
Earned versus paid: Which placements are independently editorial, and which are sponsored?
Proof: Can they provide two or three comparable TGE case studies with links to actual coverage?
Embargoes: Which journalists have agreed to consider stories before launch? No reputable firm can guarantee independent editorial coverage.
Deliverables: Who owns founder interviews, press materials, exchange announcements, crisis messaging and post-TGE follow-through?
Compliance: Will they coordinate token-related claims with your legal counsel and avoid unsupported return or price promises?
