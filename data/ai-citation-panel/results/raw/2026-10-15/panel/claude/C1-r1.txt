Highwire: Good for larger, enterprise-focused AI companies. After acquiring The Bliss Group in January 2026, it has 250+ people across North America. Typical retainers run about $15,000 to $30,000 a month. [everything-pr] [onyamagazine]

Bospar: A fully remote, independent tech PR firm founded in 2015, known for aggressive media outreach. It recently hired veteran AI reporter Clint Boulton to advise clients on pitching top-tier media. [everything-pr] [odwyerpr]

Outcast: A go-to for well-funded startups that have already found product-market fit. [everything-pr]

Treble PR: Built specifically for early-stage, VC-backed startups. [everything-pr]

Salient PR: A boutique with founder-led execution, suited to post-Series A startups. [onyamagazine]

On budget, a Series A startup should expect roughly $8,000 to $20,000 per month for a quality agency. [everything-pr]
