4. How I'd choose a provider

For a seed-stage startup: Start with a short, fixed-scope Japan campaign rather than committing immediately to a long retainer.

For a token generation event or exchange-listing campaign: Ask for a detailed plan covering Japanese media, community channels, localization and regulatory review.

For a company entering Japan long-term: Consider a local agency with Japanese-language account management and demonstrated crypto-sector experience.

For credibility with investors or institutions: Prioritize earned editorial, substantive interviews and a clear regulatory narrative over a high count of syndicated releases.
