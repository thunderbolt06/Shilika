Researched 1s

Several crypto PR firms explicitly build campaigns around the pre-TGE window - narrative seeding, community/KOL activation, investor relations, media outreach, and exchange/listing coordination before the token generation event. The clearest specialists are Coinband, Cryptic, Lunar Strategy, Omni Agency, ICODA, TokenMinds, Serotonin, and Outset PR.
