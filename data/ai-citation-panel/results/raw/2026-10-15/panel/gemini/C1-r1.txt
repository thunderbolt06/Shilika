The PR landscape for AI startups has shifted significantly. In 2026, the best agencies do not just pitch tier-one media; they also specialize in Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) - ensuring that when potential clients or investors ask AI search engines (like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity) for recommendations, your company gets cited alongside traditional press coverage.

Here are the top PR firms for AI startups, categorized by stage, budget, and specialty:

1. High Vibe PR
Best For: AI-native launches, category creation, and AI search visibility (GEO).
Key Focus: Boutique, high-touch strategy for generative AI, autonomous agents, AI infrastructure, and deep tech.
Why They Stand Out: Beyond securing coverage in outlets like TechCrunch, Bloomberg, and Business Insider, they use proprietary tools to track and optimize how your brand is cited inside ChatGPT, Gemini, and AI Overviews. They specialize in turning funding rounds into category-defining narratives rather than simple news blips.

2. Crackle PR
Best For: Series A+ B2B AI startups wanting senior-only teams.
Pricing Model: Starts around $10,000 / month (£9,400/mo).
Why They Stand Out: Operates a fully remote-first model staffed exclusively with 15+ year veteran strategists. Every engagement integrates traditional media relations, analyst relations, and LLM optimization to ensure coverage drives search visibility.

3. Sparkpr
Best For: Venture-backed deep tech and Series A+ founders needing Silicon Valley depth.
Pricing Model: Retainers typically start around $15,000 / month (£11,800/mo).
Why They Stand Out: A legacy Silicon Valley agency with 25+ years of experience that has adapted aggressively to AI. Excellent for enterprise AI, foundation models, and startups backed by top-tier VC firms that need analyst relations (Gartner/Forrester) alongside top-tier press.

4. Treble
Best For: Fast-moving Seed through Series C B2B AI startups.
Pricing Model: Flexible launch sprints starting around $8,000 / month (£6,270/mo).
Why They Stand Out: Designed specifically around startup funding cycles and product launches. They offer short, high-intensity sprints for early-stage companies and embedded senior teams for ongoing retainer work.

5. Jamco PR
Best For: Early-stage founders needing fast, guaranteed media traction.
Pricing Model: Guaranteed performance model (placements delivered within 60 days or replaced).
Why They Stand Out: Low-friction setup designed for founders who don't have time for extensive onboarding. Excellent for pre-seed/seed rounds needing quick credibility on outlets like Business Insider, Yahoo Finance, and trade press.

6. The Colab
Best For: Complex B2B AI, developer tools, and ML infrastructure.
Why They Stand Out: Their team includes former tech journalists who understand complex architectures (transformer models, fine-tuning pipelines, compute infrastructure) and can accurately translate technical depth for mainstream business press and technical publications alike.

Key Questions to Ask an AI PR Firm Before Hiring
1. How do you handle Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)? What to look for: They should have a concrete framework for structuring press releases, executive quotes, and digital footprints so that LLMs summarize your startup favorably.
2. Can you explain our technical architecture back to us? What to look for: Avoid agencies that treat AI like a buzzword. Test whether they understand the nuance between fine-tuning, RAG, and agentic workflows.
3. Do you offer launch sprints or just long-term retainers? What to look for: Seed-stage startups often only need a 6-to-8 week funding launch sprint, whereas Series A+ companies usually require ongoing thought leadership and analyst relations.
