The advantages

What a curated tour gives you that a booking spreadsheet cannot.

01

Fit over volume

Every show is matched to your actual audience: investors, protocol builders, developers or mainstream business readers. Fifty listener shows are declined. A hundred-thousand listener shows are pitched.

02

Brief preparation

Before each recording you get a show brief, a talk-track aligned to your current narrative and three talking points that make the host look good. That prep is why founders get invited back.

03

Narrative continuity

Each appearance reinforces the same category framing: the same founding story, the same insight, the same call to action. Repetition across twelve shows is how a thesis becomes received wisdom.

04

Cross-format amplification

Podcast clips are seeded to X, LinkedIn and Telegram communities. The audio becomes written quotes, which become press coverage. The Gaia AI tour produced Forbes and Decrypt articles off the back of podcast appearances.

05

AI-citation trail

Show notes, transcript pages and accompanying press index your name and category framing in the sources ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Mode pull from. Audio alone does not do this. The integrated approach does.

06

Senior operator, no coordinator overhead

You work directly with the person pitching the shows and writing the briefs. No junior coordinator learning your category on your retainer and no middlemen adding delay between decision and outreach.

How it runs

From brief to booked in four moves.

Step 01

Narrative audit

A read of your current press footprint, LinkedIn presence and past appearances to identify the one story worth pitching and the shows worth pitching it to.

Step 02

Show map

A ranked list of twenty to thirty target shows by audience fit, host credibility and booking likelihood, with a short pitch angle for each. You approve before outreach starts.

Step 03

Outreach and booking

Direct outreach to hosts and producers using warm relationships where they exist and credible cold pitches where they do not. Target: six to twelve confirmed bookings per quarter.

Step 04

Prep and amplify

Pre-recording brief for every show, post-recording clip strategy for X, LinkedIn and Telegram, and integration with any press outreach running in parallel.

FAQ

Founder podcast booking questions, answered.

What is a founder podcast booking service and who is it for?
It is a managed service that researches, pitches and books podcast appearances on your behalf, then prepares you for each recording and amplifies the output across written and social channels. It is for Web3, AI and deep-tech founders who want to build a durable public profile but do not have the time or show relationships to do it themselves. Shilika Jain runs the service directly for $5K–$12K per month, targeting six to twelve confirmed bookings per quarter. See the full scope at founder profiling and op-eds.
How much does podcast booking for founders cost?
A managed podcast tour runs $5K–$12K per month as part of a broader founder profiling retainer. A standalone quarterly sprint covering show research, outreach, bookings and prep briefs sits at the lower end of that range. Full agency equivalents charge $15K–$45K per month for the same scope with junior coordinators on the account. The sprint model, which targets one quarter with a defined show list and narrative, is typically $15K–$25K all in.
Which shows can you get me booked on?
The short answer is: shows whose audience matches your actual target, not shows with the biggest follower count. Typical placements for Web3 and AI founders include Unchained, Bankless, The Defiant, Epicenter, Web3 Breakfast, Wyre Talks and vertical shows in DePIN, RWA, AI infrastructure and cybersecurity. Mainstream business shows such as How I Built This or The Tim Ferriss Show require a crossover narrative and are pitched only when there is a genuine angle. Every show list is approved by you before outreach starts.
How long does it take to see results?
First confirmed bookings typically come within three to four weeks of outreach starting, assuming you are responsive on scheduling. A full quarter of six to twelve bookings is the standard cadence. The AI-citation and press compounding effect builds over two to three quarters as transcripts, show notes and accompanying articles index in search engines and AI training data. One quarter is useful. Three quarters is when it becomes a competitive moat.
