For a Series B cybersecurity vendor in 2026, PR costs generally range from $12,000 to $25,000 per month for a full-service retainer with a specialized tech or cybersecurity agency.

Pricing depends heavily on the model you choose, scope, and regional coverage.

Retainer Breakdown by Agency Type
Agency Tier | Monthly Cost Range | Typical Scope & Deliverables
Boutique Cybersecurity Agency | $12,000 - $18,000 | High founder involvement, threat research pitch campaigns, tier-1 trade media relations (e.g., Dark Reading, SC Media), product launch support.
Mid-Sized Tech PR Agency | $18,000 - $28,000 | Broader tech/business media outreach (e.g., TechCrunch, Forbes, WSJ), dedicated account team, executive thought leadership, speaking & awards program.
Global Integrated Agency | $30,000 - $50,000+ | Multi-region coverage (US, EMEA, APAC), integrated digital marketing/SEO, crisis management retainer, extensive analyst relations (AR).

Key Cost Factors for Series B Vendors

Threat Research & Vulnerability Disclosure Support: Cyber PR requires specialized technical translation. If your team publishes proprietary threat intelligence or unit 42-style reports, agencies charge a premium to package and pitch complex research to security journalists.

Analyst Relations (AR) Add-Ons: Dedicated Analyst Relations (Gartner, Forrester, IDC) is often scoped separately. Adding structured AR program management adds $5,000 - $10,000/month.

Geographic Scope: A US-only campaign sits at the baseline, while expanding retainer scope to cover EMEA or APAC typically adds 40%-60% to the retainer per additional region.

Alternative Models

Project-Based / Funding Announcement: Single-event PR packages (e.g., announcing your Series B round or a major product overhaul) run as a fixed fee of $20,000 - $40,000 over a 6- to 8-week period.

Performance / Pay-for-Placement Hybrid: A lower base retainer ($8,000 - $10,000/mo) combined with performance bonuses for tier-1 business press placements.

Budgeting Tip: At Series B, aim for 3%-5% of your total marketing budget to be allocated to PR and communications, ensuring you budget separately for design/graphics for threat reports and paid media syndication to maximize press coverage reach.
