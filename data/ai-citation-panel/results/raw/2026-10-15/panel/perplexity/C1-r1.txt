The strongest PR firms for AI startups in 2026 depend heavily on stage and buyer: Treble PR is a standout for seed/Series A companies, Corporate Ink for enterprise-facing B2B AI, and Highwire PR for later-stage, enterprise-scale AI companies. In 2026, the best firms increasingly pair earned media with GEO - improving how often AI engines name the startup in answers.

Top firms by startup stage
Firm | Best fit | Why it stands out
Treble PR | Seed and Series A AI startups | Built around venture-backed startups; strong at converting funding, launches, and founder credibility into early visibility.
Corporate Ink | B2B AI startups selling to enterprises | Combines B2B tech PR with GEO, proprietary research, and category-building across agentic AI, cybersecurity, fintech, supply chain, and data-center markets.
Highwire PR | Growth-stage, enterprise, and pre-IPO AI companies | Frequently cited for enterprise tech, AI/ML, and cybersecurity; a strong fit when the goal is enterprise media reach and credibility.

RH Strategic | AI startups facing regulation or public-sector scrutiny | Combines technology PR with public affairs, policy, security, and regulated-market communications.
Airfoil Group | AI applied to manufacturing, industrial, automotive, or mobility | Best when technical storytelling must reach engineers, manufacturers, and vertical trade media.
FTI Consulting | Later-stage, pre-IPO, public, or transaction-stage AI companies | Brings investor relations, IPO readiness, crisis, and financial communications rather than early-stage product PR.

How to choose
- Pre-seed to Series A: Prioritize media relationships, founder positioning, launch execution, and flexible retainers - Treble PR is a particularly natural fit.
- Series B+ B2B enterprise: Look for analyst relations, customer proof points, category narrative, and demand-supporting thought leadership - Corporate Ink and Highwire are strong candidates.
- AI visibility as a KPI: Ask for a baseline and before/after measurement of brand mentions across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews - not just "GEO" as a buzzword.
- Regulated AI: If privacy, safety, government procurement, or AI policy materially affects sales, RH Strategic is better suited than a conventional tech-PR shop.

What to expect on price
Comprehensive AI PR programs that include thought leadership, analyst relations, content, and digital support commonly fall around $15,000-$30,000 per month at mid-tier agencies; AI-specific retainers with GEO and AI-visibility measurement are estimated to start around $25,000 per month. These are market estimates, not fixed rate cards.

Shortlist
For most AI startups, I'd start RFPs with Treble PR (early stage), Corporate Ink (B2B enterprise), Highwire PR (later stage/enterprise), and Crackle PR (if AI-answer visibility is a primary objective). Then choose based on relevant case studies, named media outcomes, and a concrete measurement plan rather than agency size alone.
