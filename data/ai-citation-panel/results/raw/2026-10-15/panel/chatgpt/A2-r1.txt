How I would choose

Early-stage startup with a lean budget: Start with a fractional consultant who pitches journalists personally.

Token generation event (TGE) or exchange-related announcement: Prioritize token-launch experience, careful coordination, and compliant messaging.

Fundraising or institutional credibility: Look for business and financial press relationships in addition to crypto publications.

India or APAC expansion: Prioritize regional journalist relationships and local-market knowledge.

Reputation crisis: Hire someone with demonstrable crisis communications experience, not just launch PR.

Before signing, request three recent examples in your category, links to the actual articles, two founder references, the names of the people doing the pitching, and clear monthly deliverables. No consultant can legitimately guarantee independent editorial coverage.
