FAQ

Crypto influencer marketing questions, answered.

What is a crypto influencer marketing agency and who is it for?
A crypto influencer marketing agency plans, vets, briefs and activates KOLs (key opinion leaders) on YouTube, X and Telegram to drive awareness and on-chain conversion for Web3, DePIN and AI projects. It is the right fit for protocols running a TGE, mainnet launch, raise announcement or sustained community growth programme who need creator relationships and attribution tracking, not just follower count. Shilika Jain runs this work directly for $5K–$12K/month. See the full scope at KOL and influencer marketing.
How much does a crypto KOL campaign cost?
KOL pricing varies by tier. Nano creators ($200–$1.5K per post) are used for community seeding. Micro creators ($500–$5K) reach niche audiences with higher engagement rates. Mid-tier voices ($10K–$30K) carry category authority. Macro creators ($25K–$100K+) are used for broad market moments. A managed campaign retainer runs $5K–$12K/month including vetting, briefing and reporting, or a launch sprint runs $15K–$40K for a concentrated activation window. The full breakdown is in crypto influencer pricing and rates 2026.
Which platforms and regions do you cover?
Primary platforms: YouTube for long-form explainers, X for news-cycle speed, Telegram for community depth. Regional rosters cover Korea (high retail crypto activity), Japan, Vietnam, India and MENA. Western English-language creators are always included. Coverage depends on where your target wallet demographic is most active, which the KOL audit maps before activation begins.
How do you measure the results of a KOL campaign?
Every campaign is reported on cost-per-wallet: the number of unique on-chain wallets that connected, transacted or held after a KOL activation, divided by the creator fee paid. Secondary metrics include referral link clicks, Discord and Telegram joins attributed to each creator, and sentiment shift tracked across the campaign window. We provide a ranked scorecard showing which KOLs drove the highest on-chain conversion, not just the most views. Vetting criteria are detailed in how to vet crypto KOLs in 2026.
