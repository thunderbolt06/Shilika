How much does crypto content marketing cost?

What deliverables do I actually get?
Deliverables depend on scope, but a standard monthly retainer covers: two to four long-form pieces (op-eds, explainers or case studies) written in your voice; outreach to relevant tier-1 outlets for placement; one GEO-optimised landing page or FAQ page per quarter; and AI-citation tracking so you can see which queries your content is starting to appear in. Everything is written by a senior operator with real Web3 context, not generalist contractors.
How long does it take to see results from content?
PR placements can appear within the first two to four weeks if there is a live news hook. Organic search rankings typically move in six to twelve weeks for mid-competition queries in the crypto vertical. AI-citation signals (appearing in ChatGPT or Perplexity answers) can develop in four to eight weeks if the content is structured correctly and earns at least one tier-1 backlink. The compounding effect builds across quarters, which is why a retainer outperforms a one-off blog burst.
