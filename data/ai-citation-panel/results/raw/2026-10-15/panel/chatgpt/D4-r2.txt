For a Series B cybersecurity vendor, a realistic US-market budget is $15,000-$25,000 per month for a full-service specialist PR agency, with approximately $20,000-$25,000 per month a sensible planning target for a company seeking sustained enterprise visibility, analyst engagement, and executive thought leadership. Specialist cybersecurity pricing guides place growth-stage programs in this range, while some traditional agencies serving Series A and B companies quote $20,000-$35,000 monthly. [chip: SecuritySenses +2]

That translates to roughly $180,000-$300,000 per year before additional campaign expenses.

1. 2026 pricing benchmarks
Fractional cybersecurity PR - Senior consultant; focused scope, fewer parallel workstreams - $5K-$12K per month
Boutique specialist agency - Security trade media, executive profiles, basic analyst engagement - $8K-$15K per month
Series B full-service program - Media relations, analyst relations, thought leadership, research amplification - $15K-$25K per month
Enterprise / global PR - Multiple regions, crisis readiness, intensive analyst and executive programs - $25K-$50K+ per month
Sources: 2026 cybersecurity and technology PR pricing guides. These are indicative market estimates, not independently audited industry averages. [chip: SecuritySenses +3]

2. What should a Series B vendor get for $20K-$25K/month?
At this budget, I would expect a strategic communications program rather than a press-release factory.
Workstream | Expected scope
Media relations | Proactive pitching to cybersecurity, enterprise technology, and selected business press
Executive visibility | Founder/CEO positioning, contributed articles, interviews and commentary
Analyst relations | Briefings and relationship development with relevant Gartner, Forrester, IDC or specialist analysts
Research-led PR | Turning threat intelligence, product telemetry or original research into newsworthy stories
Company announcements | Product launches, customer wins where publishable, partnerships and funding milestones
Measurement | Quality of coverage, competitive share of voice, message pull-through and business relevance
Crisis readiness | Communications planning for security incidents, vulnerability disclosures and sensitive events
The precise deliverables depend on the agency and contract. Analyst relations, original research production, crisis retainers and paid distribution may be additional charges. [chip: Gabriel Marketing Group +2]

3. Annual budget scenarios
Annual agency fees by monthly retainer (chart): Fractional ($8K/mo) $96K; Boutique ($12K/mo) $144K; Growth ($20K/mo) $240K; Premium ($25K/mo) $300K; Global ($40K/mo) $480K.
For planning purposes, I would budget an additional 10-20% contingency for non-retainer expenses, subject to the actual scope.
For a $20,000/month engagement, that gives: Annual retainer $240,000; Contingency $24,000-$48,000; Indicative annual budget $264,000-$288,000.
This contingency is a planning assumption, not a published industry average. Confirm whether wire distribution, research, events, awards, monitoring tools and travel are included.

4. How I would choose an agency
For a Series B company, the right price depends on what the business needs PR to accomplish over the next two to four quarters.
Choose $8K-$12K/month if the immediate priority is establishing a consistent media presence and the internal team can own strategy and execution support.
Choose $15K-$25K/month if you need repeatable earned media, executive visibility, research-led campaigns and meaningful analyst engagement.
Consider $25K+/month if you're pursuing category leadership, international expansion, multiple analyst programs or substantial crisis and corporate communications support.
Before signing, ask for named senior staff, a 90-day plan, relevant cybersecurity references, clear deliverables and a measurement framework. Avoid paying a premium solely for promised top-tier placements; earned coverage cannot be guaranteed.
