Key Nuances in the Campaign Strategy

1. Regulatory Alignment: Because of India's ongoing crypto tax frameworks and data compliance standards (such as the Digital Personal Data Protection Act for AI models), campaigns carry regulatory-aware framing tailored for local business journalists.

2. Developer-First Narratives: Many PR pushes leverage major ecosystem events-such as ETHIndia, NamasteWeb3, and regional tech summits-to break product announcements and land exclusive profiles.

3. Cross-Border Framing (US / APAC / India): For Indian founders catering to global markets, PR strategists pair US/global tech press for product credibility with Indian tier-1 outlets for talent recruitment and investor presence.
