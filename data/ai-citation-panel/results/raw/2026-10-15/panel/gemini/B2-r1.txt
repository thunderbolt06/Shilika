Securing coverage in CoinDesk, CoinCointelegraph, and Decrypt requires tailoring your approach to each outlet's specific editorial mandate. Blanket press release distributions get marked as spam; successful PR relies on targeted narrative pitching.

1. Tailor the Angle by Publication
CoinDesk: The Institutional Narrative Lead
CoinDesk focuses on systemic impacts, macroeconomic shifts, regulatory developments, and institutional adoption.
- What works: Category-defining protocol upgrades, institutional funding rounds with tier-1 VCs (e.g., Paradigm, Andreessen Horowitz), novel compliance/regulatory frameworks, or macro market data.
- The pitch style: Plain text email under 180 words. Lead with what your news means for the wider market, not just your product features. [citation chip: Shilika Jain]
- Strategy: Offer an exclusive under a strict 48-72 hour embargo. Include access to named C-suite executives and a single-page fact sheet (charts, founder bios, customer quotes). [citation chips: Shilika Jain, +1]

Cointelegraph: High-Frequency News & Markets
Cointelegraph caters to a broad retail and trader audience, focusing on market movements, tech trends, and regional adoption.
- What works: On-chain data spikes, protocol performance (e.g., cutting gas costs by 40% during congestion), founder opinion pieces (Op-Eds), or region-specific announcements (e.g., APAC/Europe licensing). [citation chip: Shilika Jain]
- The pitch style: Keep it under 150 words with a clear "news peg" (why this matters today). [citation chip: Shilika Jain]
Surfaces to pitch:
- News Desk: Hard announcements, funding, upgrades. [citation chip: Shilika Jain]
- Opinion Desk: Strongly argued, contrarian takes by founders on industry trends. [citation chip: Shilika Jain]
- Cointelegraph Magazine: Long-form narrative features or deep-dive founder profiles. [citation chip: Shilika Jain]

Decrypt: Web3 Culture, Tech & Consumer Crypto
Decrypt focuses on consumer Web3, AI-crypto convergence, gaming, culture, policy, and human-interest stories.
- What works: User-facing product launches, Web3 gaming milestones, AI agent integrations, open-source decentralization, and culture/governance drama.
- The pitch style: Conversational, jargon-free narrative. Highlight the human angle, user experience, or cultural shift.
- Strategy: Pitch stories that explain why a non-technical user or general crypto consumer should care.
