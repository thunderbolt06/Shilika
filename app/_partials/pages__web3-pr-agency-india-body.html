How much does India Web3 PR cost in 2026?
A global agency with an India desk typically charges $15K–$45K/month because the retainer funds multiple account teams. A fractional senior operator covering both India and global crypto outlets runs at $5K–$12K/month. Which Indian outlets can you actually get a Web3 project into?
India startup and business press: Inc42, YourStory, Economic Times Tech, Business Standard, Entrackr and Livemint where a financial angle holds. Global crypto from the same launch: CoinDesk, Cointelegraph, Decrypt, The Block, Blockworks and Forbes where the hook warrants a mainstream run. Coverage depends on a real news hook pitched to the right editor, not a wire blast or a press-release submission form.
What does the dual-track launch process look like and how long does it take?
The dual-track model runs a single news event, for example a seed raise or TGE, through two simultaneous pitching tracks: one angled for India startup press and one angled for global crypto media. Preparation takes three to four weeks: narrative drafting, journalist relationship activation, embargo agreements and India KOL coordination. The launch window runs 48 hours.
